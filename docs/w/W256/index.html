Records / W256
Written source · French · French and Dutch

General history of China, or Annals of that empire, translated from the Tongjian gangmu (13 vols, 1777-1785); vols 9 and 11 cover the Yuan (Mongol) and Qing (Manchu) periods

Joseph de Mailla, translator; Grosier and Deshauterayes, editors

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier sources: translation of the Manchu version of the Tongjian gangmu continuation, with the Ming and Qing portions compiled by Mailla from other Chinese works; Mailla resident in Peking 1701-1748

Cautions
  • Mailla translated a Manchu version and interpolated; Grosier edited posthumously; the Qing section (vol. 11) is Mailla's own compilation, not the Gangmu. English pass W098 (Grosier) is the abridged companion.
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/A209053

Why it matters

The only full pre-1850 European rendering of the Chinese annalistic record for the Mongol and Manchu dynasties: it is where French readers got the Chinese view of the Yuan and of the Qing conquest of Mongolia and Dzungaria. Grosier's Description (W098) is its companion volume.
