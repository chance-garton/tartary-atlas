Abd al-Razzaq Samarqandi's Rising of the Two Auspicious Stars

A Timurid history of 1304 to 1470 by a court historian who also travelled as an envoy.

Abd al-Razzaq Samarqandi (1413-1482)

Open the original book

Abd al-Razzaq Samarqandi (1413 to 1482) wrote this Persian history around 1467 to 1471. For 1304 to 1427 he draws on Hafiz-i Abru and others. For Shahrukh's later reign he writes from experience, including his own embassy to India in 1442 to 1445. It is the main Timurid source for the fifteenth-century Uzbek and Moghul steppe and for embassies to Ming China. The atlas does not hold the text: the corpus knows a modern digital retypesetting of the Tehran edition, and it keeps full text only for works published before 1929 or openly licensed.
