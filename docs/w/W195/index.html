Rising of the Two Auspicious Stars and Junction of the Two Seas: Timurid history 1304-1470, Persian text (digital reissue of the Tehran edition, Ghaemiyeh, Isfahan)

مطلع سعدین و مجمع بحرین

Abd al-Razzaq Samarqandi (1413-1482)

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

mixed: compilation of Hafiz-i Abru and others for 1304-1427; firsthand for Shahrukh's later reign and the author's own embassies (India 1442-1445)

Cautions
  • A digital re-typesetting, not a scan of the printed edition; verify readings against Nava'i's printed volumes (Tehran 1372-1383 SH) or the Lahore 1941-49 edition.

Why it matters

Main Timurid source for the fifteenth-century Uzbek (Abu'l-Khayrid) and Moghul steppe, and for Shahrukh's embassies to Ming China; the source of the Quatremere and Major (India in the Fifteenth Century, W060) extracts.
