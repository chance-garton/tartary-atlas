Written source · Ottoman Turkish · Persian, Arabic, Ottoman, Chagatai and Tatar

Book of Travels, volumes 7 and 8 (Crimea, the Caucasus, the Kalmyk lands, the Volga, Azov, the Balkans), Istanbul: Turk Tarih Encumeni / Devlet Matbaasi, 1928

اولیا چلبی سیاحتنامه‌سی، جلد ۷ و ۸

Evliya Celebi (1611-c.1685)

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation of the 1660s journeys (Crimea 1665-1666, Kalmyk and Volga lands, Azov, Caucasus) mixed with hearsay, literary embellishment and invented episodes; the author's veracity varies by passage

Cautions
  • IA catalogue date 1896 conflicts with the 1928 imprint of vols 7-8 (preface and colophon confirm 1928). The 1928 edition is censored and uncritical; the Yapi Kredi transcription (2003) and the Topkapi manuscript are the scholarly base. Evliya's Volga-Kazan itinerary and some Kalmyk passages are considered invented or exaggerated.

Why it matters

The most extensive Ottoman eyewitness description of Crimea, the Nogay and Kalmyk steppe and the Volga in the 1660s, including the famous (and partly fictional) journey through the Kalmyk lands; a key non-European voice on 'Little Tartary'.
