Contributions to the Topographical Knowledge of the Russian Empire, 3 volumes (vol. 1 itinerary Orenburg steppe and Siberia; vol. 3 the peoples: Tatars, Kalmyks, Kirgis-Kaisaks)

Johann Peter Falck; J. G. Georgi (ed.)

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation 1768-1774 on the Orenburg-Siberian Academy expedition (Falck died 1774 at Kazan); arranged posthumously by Georgi

Cautions
  • Georgi compiled from Falck's incomplete papers and added material; the third volume on peoples overlaps Georgi's own Beschreibung aller Nationen.

Why it matters

Contains the fullest eighteenth-century German ethnography of the Kirgis-Kaisak hordes and of the Bukharan traders of Siberia, and a systematic topography of the Orenburg frontier line; complements Pallas and Rychkov.
