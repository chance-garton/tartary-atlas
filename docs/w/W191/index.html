History of Muqim Khan (Tarikh-i Muqim-khani)

A Bukharan chronicle of the Ashtarkhanid khans, from the sixteenth century to 1704.

Full title History of Muqim Khan: Uzbek (Ashtarkhanid) history of Bukhara and Balkh, Russian translation by A. A. Semenov, Tashkent 1956

تاریخ مقیم خانی

Muhammad Yusuf Munshi (fl. 1697-1704)

Open the original book

Muhammad Yusuf Munshi wrote this Persian history around 1697 to 1704. It opens with a retrospective run through Chinggisid and Shibanid history, then gives a contemporary account of Subhan Quli Khan and Muhammad Muqim Khan of Balkh in the 1690s and early 1700s, with Uzbek, Kazakh and Qalmaq relations seen from inside. The atlas does not hold the text: the corpus knows only A. A. Semenov's 1956 Russian translation, as a web transcription, and no critical Persian text exists.
