Description of All the Nations of the Russian Empire, their way of life, religion, customs, dwellings, clothing and other remarkable features, 4 parts with coloured plates (part 2: Tatar nations; part 4: Mongolian nations)

Johann Gottlieb Georgi

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of Academy expedition results (Pallas, Falck, Lepekhin, Gmelin, Müller, Steller) with Georgi's own observations 1770-1774

Cautions
  • Secondary compilation; Georgi rarely names his sources per entry. English version (IA bim_eighteenth-century_beschreibung-aller-nati_georgi 1780-1783) not in the English pass.
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/10691431bsb; archive.org/details/10691432bsb

Why it matters

Codifies the eighteenth-century classification of the empire's peoples into Tatar and Mongol nations with a plate for each; the explicit statement that Tatar and nomad polities are democratic rather than monarchic appears in the sample. Widely translated (English Russia, or a Compleat Historical Account 1780-1783).
