Qi Yunshi's Essentials of the Dependent Territories

A Qing annalistic history of how each Mongol, Oirat, Tibetan and Muslim region came under the dynasty.

Full title Essentials of the Dependent Territories of the Reigning Dynasty (Inner and Outer Mongols, Oirats, Qinghai, Tibet, Muslim regions), with genealogical tables

皇朝藩部要略; 皇朝藩部世系表

Qi Yunshi (1751-1815); edited and printed by Zhang Mu 1839-1846

Open the original book

Qi Yunshi (1751 to 1815), an archivist of the Grand Secretariat, compiled this work about 1807, arranged as annals for 1583 to 1795 with genealogical tables. Zhang Mu edited and printed it between 1839 and 1846. It covers Inner and Outer Mongolia, the Oirats and Dzungaria, Qinghai, Tibet and the Muslim regions. The card calls it the Qing definition of the dependencies that European maps labelled Chinese Tartary. The atlas holds only a poor-quality machine reading (OCR) of this scan, and no passages were selected or read for this guide.

How they knew
Compiled from others
Who “Tartar” means here
Not recorded. The card names the Khalkha, Chakhar, Khorchin, the Oirats (Dzungar, Torghut, Khoshut), Tibetans and Turkic Muslims. It ties the work to the regions European maps labelled Chinese Tartary.
Why read it
It gives the Qing administrative view, group by group, of the territories that European maps lumped together as Tartary.
Watch out
It is a digest of official records, and Zhang Mu's edition adds corrections. The card says Qi Yunshi's Xichui yaolue of 1807 was not found open.
