Essentials of the Dependent Territories of the Reigning Dynasty (Inner and Outer Mongols, Oirats, Qinghai, Tibet, Muslim regions), with genealogical tables

皇朝藩部要略; 皇朝藩部世系表

Qi Yunshi (1751-1815); edited and printed by Zhang Mu 1839-1846

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

official-archival compilation by a Grand Secretariat archivist who had worked on the Menggu Huibu wanggong biaozhuan, arranged as annals 1583-1795

Cautions
  • Digest of the Waifan Menggu Huibu wanggong biaozhuan and shilu; Zhang Mu's edition adds corrections. Qi Yunshi's Xichui yaolüe (1807) not located open.

Why it matters

Annalistic Qing account of the submission of each Mongol and Oirat group with genealogies: the Qing administrative definition of 'the dependencies' that European maps labelled Chinese Tartary.
