The embassy of the Dutch East India Company to the Great Tartar Khan, the present emperor of China (1655-1657 embassy of De Goyer and De Keyser)

Johan Nieuhof

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation (Nieuhof was steward of the embassy) for the journey; second part is compilation from Martini, Trigault and others; text reworked by his brother Hendrik and publisher Van Meurs

Cautions
  • Printed text heavily edited by Hendrik Nieuhof and Van Meurs; compare with the 1659 autograph journal (recorded separately). French (1665), German (1669), Latin (1668) and English (Ogilby 1669) versions derive from it; MDZ has the French 1665 (bsb11197883, bsb11197884) and German 1669 (bsb11448670).
  • Second part (Algemeene beschryving van het ryk Sina) is compilation, not observation; keep the two parts distinct when citing.
  • Further copies: hdl.handle.net/10366/162450 (Latin 1668, Salamanca)

Why it matters

The best-selling early European description of Qing China at the moment of Manchu ("Tartar") conquest; its title equates the Qing emperor with the Great Tartar Khan, fixing the vocabulary later writers (Witsen, Dapper) inherited. Dedicated to Cornelis Witsen, father of Nicolaas.
