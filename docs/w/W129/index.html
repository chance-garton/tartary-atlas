The Blue Annals (completed 1476-1478)

དེབ་ཐེར་སྔོན་པོ།

Gö Lotsawa Zhönnu Pel (1392-1481)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective history compiled from earlier Tibetan records, with the author's own recollections for the 15th century

Cautions
  • Undated block print; compare Lokesh Chandra's 1974 reproduction (BDRC W7494, also open, 970 canvases) and Roerich's English translation (1949-53, IA blueannals01gosl).

Why it matters

Principal Tibetan historical compendium; its chronology of Mongol patronage of Tibetan schools (13th-14th c.) is an independent check on Yuan-period dates in the Chinese and Mongolian records.
