Written source · German · German

History of the Golden Horde in Kipchak, that is, of the Mongols in Russia (Pest 1840), with nine appendices of translated Arabic, Persian and Turkish sources and a coin plate

Joseph von Hammer-Purgstall

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective history compiled from Arabic and Persian historians (Ibn Battuta, al-Umari, Rashid al-Din, Juvaini, Mirkhwand and others), Russian chronicles in translation, and coins; prize essay for the St Petersburg Academy

Cautions
  • Rejected by the St Petersburg Academy (Frähn's critique) for philological errors; Hammer published it independently with the correspondence. Translations are Hammer's own and often loose.

Why it matters

First monographic history of the Golden Horde in any western language, giving German readers direct extracts from the Islamic sources on the Mongols in Russia; documents which oriental texts were available in 1840 and how the Horde was distinguished from Tartary at large.
