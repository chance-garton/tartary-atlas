Raguzinsky's secret report on China (1731)

A Russian ambassador's private assessment of Qing power, written for Empress Anna in 1731.

Full title Secret information on the strength and condition of the Chinese state (report to Empress Anna, 1731) and Note on China

Секретная информация о силе и состоянии Китайского государства (1731); Записка о Китае

Count Sava Vladislavich-Raguzinsky, Russian ambassador

Open the original book

Count Sava Vladislavich-Raguzinsky negotiated the Treaty of Kiakhta in 1727 and travelled through Mongolia in 1725 to 1728. His secret report to Empress Anna in 1731, with a shorter Note on China, sizes up the strength of the Qing state and the Dzungar-Qing war zone. It touches Mongolia, the Kiakhta frontier, Beijing and Manchuria. The atlas does not hold the text: the card names two nineteenth-century Russian web texts (1842 and 1900), and nothing is stored in the corpus.
