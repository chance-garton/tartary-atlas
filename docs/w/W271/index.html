Records / W271
Written source · Latin texts with French apparatus · French and Dutch

Collection of voyages and memoirs published by the Geographical Society, vol. 4 (1839): the first critical Latin editions of Carpini’s Historia Mongalorum and Rubruck’s Itinerarium, with Jordanus’ Mirabilia and other medieval texts

Societe de Geographie; d'Avezac, Michel and Wright, editors

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand 13th-century mission reports (Carpini 1245-47, Rubruck 1253-55) and 14th-century Jordanus, presented in Latin from manuscripts with French scholarly introductions and notes

Cautions
  • Critical edition from limited manuscripts; d'Avezac's Carpini text superseded by Wyngaert (1929) but remains the first. IA volume field reads "Tom 4" with no date in one copy; DLI copy dated 1839.
  • Other volumes of the Recueil (1824-1866) include Idrisi (Jaubert translation, vols 5-6, 1836-1840) and Marco Polo (vol. 1, 1824, Roux edition): both relevant to Tartary geography and located on IA (recueildevoyages51soci, recueildevoyages62soci, india.history.resource.111340).

Why it matters

The scholarly Latin base for Carpini and Rubruck before Beazley and Wyngaert; d'Avezac's introduction is the first full study of the Mongol mission texts and their manuscripts, so this volume is the transmission check for every later translation of Carpini and Rubruck (W043 in English, Bergeron in French).
