Посольство к зюнгарскому хун-тайчжи Цэван Рабтану капитана от артиллерии Ивана Унковского и путевой журнал его за 1722-1724 годы

Captain Ivan Unkovsky; ed. N. I. Veselovsky

firsthand journal of residence at the Dzungar court on the Ili, with route from Tobolsk via Irtysh and Zaisan

Cautions

Why it matters

The most detailed 18th century Russian eyewitness record of the Dzungar khanate at its height, a state often folded into the notion of Tartary.
