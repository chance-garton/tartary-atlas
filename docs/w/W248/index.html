Records / W248
Written source · Dutch · French and Dutch

Memorable deeds of the Dutch East India Company on the coast and in the empire of Taising or China (second and third Dutch embassies, 1662-1663 Bort fleet and 1666-1668 Van Hoorn embassy to Kangxi), with a description of all China

Olfert Dapper

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier sources: Dapper never travelled; he worked up VOC journals (Balthasar Bort, Pieter van Hoorn, Olfert Dapper used Constantijn Nobel and others) and Jesuit works (Martini, Kircher)

Cautions
  • Compilation; embassy journals are transmitted through Dapper, not printed independently. English version in Ogilby, Atlas Chinensis (1671) misattributed to Montanus.
  • Witsen cites Dapper-derived Niuche material; Ogilby 1671 English translation exists for cross-checking.

Why it matters

Records the Dutch alliance with the Qing ("Tartars") against the Zheng regime and the 1666-68 embassy to Kangxi; its geographic part treats Manchuria as Oost-Tartarye, one of the sources Witsen excerpts. Shows how "Tartar" was used for the Manchu state in Dutch commercial reporting.
