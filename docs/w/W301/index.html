General Northern History, from the newest and best northern writers and own investigations, as a geographical and historical introduction to a correct knowledge of all Scandinavian, Finnish, Slavic, Lettish and Siberian peoples (Halle 1771; copy dated 1785 in catalogue)

August Ludwig Schlözer

How we know

compilation of earlier sources (Strahlenberg, Müller, Fischer, Herberstein, Russian chronicles) with critical discussion; no firsthand travel

Cautions
  • Published as vol 31 of the Allgemeine Welthistorie continuation; catalogue date 1785 versus known first edition 1771, record both.

Why it matters

The Göttingen synthesis that reorganized the peoples of the Russian north and Siberia by language family and explicitly attacked loose Tatar and Scythian genealogies; a benchmark for late-eighteenth-century German scholarly usage.
