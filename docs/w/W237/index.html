Description of Tartary, never before published, with a map of the Tauric Chersonese (Crimea); bound with Reichersdorff’s Transylvania and Moldavia

Marcin Broniewski (Martin Broniovius), twice Polish envoy to the Crimean khan

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation: Broniewski was envoy of Stephen Báthory to Khan Mehmed II Giray in 1578-1579, describing the khanate's towns, ruins, court, army and economy; written c. 1579, printed 1595

Cautions
  • Published posthumously by the Cologne press together with Reichersdorff; English translation in Purchas (1625) abridged; Russian translation 1867.
  • MAPS TEAM: folded 'tabula geographica Chersonesus Tauricae' within this volume.

Why it matters

The most detailed sixteenth-century eyewitness Latin description of the Crimean khanate and its antiquities, explicitly titled 'Tartariae descriptio'; the map of Crimea is an early cartographic source (note for maps team).
