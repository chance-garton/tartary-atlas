History of the Tartars (the ‘Tartar Relation’), Beinecke MS 350A.1

Brother C. de Bridia

How we know

informant testimony Written down from what informants reported.

Card wording

informant testimony: written 1247 from the oral reports of Carpini's companions (Benedict the Pole) at the request of a Franciscan provincial; surviving copy c. 1440s

Cautions
  • Text derives from the same mission as Carpini's Ystoria but is a separate redaction; only one manuscript known; authenticity of the manuscript text itself is generally accepted while the accompanying Vinland Map is disputed. Editions: Skelton, Marston and Painter 1965 (IA vinlandmaptartar00skel, lending) and Önnerfors 1967 (not open).
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/vinlandmaptartar00skel (1965 edition with facsimile and English translation, lending only)

Why it matters

Independent thirteenth-century Latin account of Mongol history and customs parallel to Carpini, with details (e.g. the 1241 Hungarian campaign, Mongol tribal names) not in Carpini; famous for being bound with the disputed Vinland Map.
