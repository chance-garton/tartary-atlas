The Golden Chronicle of Hohenschwangau, appendix on the Mongols: Latin letters of 1241 from a Bavarian manuscript

Joseph von Hormayr, editor; anonymous and named correspondents of 1241

How we know

informant testimony Written down from what informants reported.

Card wording

contemporary informant testimony: letters written in 1241 reporting the Tartar advance from Hungary toward Bohemia and the Empire; edited 1842 from a manuscript collection

Cautions
  • Hormayr's editorial reliability is uneven; letters' authorship partly anonymous; presence of Julian's 'Epistola de vita Tartarorum' in this appendix was NOT confirmed by OCR sampling (see candidate). Modern edition of the corpus: Dörrie 1956.

Why it matters

Contemporary Latin correspondence documenting the 1241 Mongol invasion as it was perceived in Central Europe; the collection is one of the vehicles for the Hungarian Dominican Julian's letter on the Tartars (not verified in this sample) and the Ottobeuren letters.
