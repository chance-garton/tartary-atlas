The Empire of China and the Evangelical Culture in it by the Jesuits (Spanish first edition of Semedo’s Relação, written in Portuguese)

Álvaro Semedo, Portuguese Jesuit; Manuel de Faria e Sousa, editor

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation (Semedo lived in China 1613-1637) with compilation of Jesuit records; Faria e Sousa edited the Portuguese manuscript into Spanish

Cautions
  • Spanish text is a translation-edition by Faria e Sousa; Portuguese original lost; the Italian 1643 Relatione differs (includes Tartar war material).
  • Portuguese Jesuit 'Cartas' on the Manchu conquest (e.g. Relação da guerra dos Tartaros) not located open this pass; see candidates.

Why it matters

Standard pre-conquest Jesuit description of Ming China with the Great Wall and the Tartar frontier on the eve of the Manchu conquest; the Italian 1643 edition added a report on the Tartar war.
