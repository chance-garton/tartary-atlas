Written source · German · German

Siberian History from the Discovery of Siberia to the Conquest of this Land by Russian Arms, read in the sessions of the Academy of Sciences, 2 volumes

Johann Eberhard Fischer

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective history compiled from Müller's archival extracts and Fischer's own participation in the Second Kamchatka Expedition (1740-1747); includes an introduction on the origin of the Tatar, Mongol and Finno-Ugric peoples

Cautions
  • Heavily dependent on Müller's unpublished Russian history; Müller accused Fischer of appropriation. A Russian translation appeared 1774 (IA SibirskajaistorijassamagootkrytijaSibiri12).
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/Sibirskajaistorijassamagoo…

Why it matters

The standard eighteenth-century synthesis of the Russian conquest of Siberia and, in its long introduction, an early comparative-ethnographic attempt to sort out who the Tatars, Mongols and Kalmyks were; frequently plagiarizes Müller, so must be read against the Sammlung.
