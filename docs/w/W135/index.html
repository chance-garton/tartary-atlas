Record of the Sacred Military Exploits (military history of the Qing, 14 juan; first edition 1842, revised 1844, 1846)

聖武記

Wei Yuan (1794-1857)

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation from Qing official campaign histories (fanglüe) and shilu, by a private scholar with access to the Grand Secretariat archives

Cautions
  • Secondary compilation from official campaign histories; three editions differ. Palladius's 1848 Russian extracts on vostlit represent the first edition.

Why it matters

The standard mid-Qing narrative of how Mongolia, Dzungaria and Xinjiang were incorporated, including the Russian frontier and the Torghut migration; a Chinese synthesis contemporaneous with the last European 'Tartary' maps.
