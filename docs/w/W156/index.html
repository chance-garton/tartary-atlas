The Clear Mirror of Royal Genealogies (1368)

A Tibetan royal history by a Sakya hierarch, ending with the Sakya and Mongol period.

Full title The Clear Mirror of Royal Genealogies (1368): history of the Tibetan kings with a closing section on the Sakya-Mongol period

རྒྱལ་རབས་གསལ་བའི་མེ་ལོང་།

Sakya Lama Dampa Sönam Gyaltsen (1312-1375)

Open the original book

Sakya Lama Dampa Sonam Gyaltsen (1312 to 1375) wrote this history of the Tibetan kings in 1368 at Samye, in the last year of Yuan rule in China. It runs from legend to his own day and closes with the Sakya and Mongol period, when the Sakya lamas dealt with the Mongol court of Godan and Qubilai. The card lists a 1973 Delhi reproduction of a manuscript, scanned at BDRC, and gives Sorensen's 1994 translation as the standard English version. The atlas does not hold the text of this edition: no copy was harvested into the corpus, so nothing from it was read for this guide.

Who “Tartar” means here
Not recorded. The card names the Mongols of the Sakya and Yuan period, including Godan and Qubilai.
Why read it
It is a near contemporary Tibetan witness to the Sakya and Mongol relationship and to the end of Yuan rule.
Watch out
The early periods are largely legendary, while the late sections are near contemporary. The copy is a manuscript reproduction, not a critical edition.
