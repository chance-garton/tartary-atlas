Records / W215
Written source · Russian · Persian, Arabic, Ottoman, Chagatai and Tatar

Book of Chinggis: history of the Jochid ulus from Chinggis Khan to Toqtamish, written in Khorezm c.1550 from oral steppe tradition; Russian translation with facsimile and transcription by V. P. Yudin (Alma-Ata 1992)

چنگیز نامه (تاريخ دوست سلطان)

Otemish Hajji ibn Mawlana Muhammad Dosti (fl. 1550)

How we know

informant testimony Written down from what informants reported.

Card wording

informant testimony: the author states he collected the accounts from elders and 'those who know the past'; retrospective for the thirteenth-fourteenth centuries

Cautions
  • Oral tradition recorded two centuries after the events; Yudin's edition used a defective manuscript. The 'continuator' text (vostlit Utemis_hadzi_prod) concerns later Shibanids.

Why it matters

The principal steppe-internal (Turkic, oral-tradition) history of the Golden Horde, independent of the Persian and Arabic court sources; central to debates on the White and Blue Hordes and on Uzbek Khan's conversion.
