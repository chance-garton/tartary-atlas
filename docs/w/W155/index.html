The Source of Scholars: Tibetan-Mongolian terminological dictionary compiled for the Mongolian translation of the Tanjur (1741-1742)

Card wording

official lexicographic compilation under Qing imperial patronage

Cautions
  • This edition prints the Tibetan only; the bilingual Beijing block print (1742) and the Mongolian-Tibetan dictionary of Sumatiratna (BDRC W1PD95802, 1959 Ulaanbaatar edition of a later 19th-century work, out of scope) are separate witnesses.

Why it matters

Standardised the Tibetan-Mongolian technical vocabulary used in 18th-19th-century Mongolian and Tibetan histories; a tool for the trilingual name register.
