Changkya Rolpe Dorje's Source of Scholars (1741 to 1742)

Catalogue title The Source of Scholars: Tibetan-Mongolian terminological dictionary compiled for the Mongolian translation of the Tanjur (1741-1742)

དག་ཡིག་མཁས་པའི་འབྱུང་གནས།

Changkya Rölpé Dorjé (1717-1786), imperial preceptor at the Qing court

Changkya Rolpe Dorje (1717 to 1786), imperial preceptor at the Qing court, compiled this dictionary of terms in 1741 to 1742 for the Mongolian translation of the Tanjur, the Tibetan Buddhist collection of commentaries. It fixed the technical vocabulary that later Tibetan and Mongolian writers used. The card lists a modern Serthar edition of 352 pages that prints the Tibetan only, scanned at BDRC. The bilingual Beijing block print of 1742 is the original form. The atlas does not hold the text of this edition: no copy was harvested into the corpus, so nothing from it was read for this guide.

Who “Tartar” means here
Not recorded, and the word is not expected in a dictionary of Buddhist and technical terms. The card names Tibetans, Mongols and the Qing court.
Why read it
It is a tool for reading the shared Tibetan and Mongolian vocabulary of the eighteenth century, alongside the trilingual name register.
Watch out
This edition prints only the Tibetan, so the Mongolian half of the original is missing. It is a reference work and holds no narrative about Tartars.

Cautions

Why it matters

Standardised the Tibetan-Mongolian technical vocabulary used in 18th-19th-century Mongolian and Tibetan histories; a tool for the trilingual name register.
