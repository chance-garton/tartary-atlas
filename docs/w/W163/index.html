Hypatian Chronicle (Complete Collection of Russian Chronicles, vol. 2): Primary Chronicle, Kievan Chronicle, Galician-Volhynian Chronicle

Ипатьевская летопись (Полное собрание русских летописей, т. 2)

Anonymous Kievan and Galician-Volhynian chroniclers; Archaeographic Commission edition

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation; Galician-Volhynian portion contemporary for 1205-1292 including Batu invasion, Daniil of Galicia at the Horde, Nogai and Telebuga campaigns

Cautions
  • Kievan chronicle sections share sources with Laurentian; Galician-Volhynian chronicle originally undated, years inserted by later copyist (chronology disputed, see Hrushevsky); 1845 edition superseded by 1908 Shakhmatov edition (also on IA, 1923 reprint).

Why it matters

Southern Rus perspective on the Mongol invasion and the Horde, independent of the Suzdal tradition; source for Rus-Tatar-Hungarian-Polish relations in the 13th century.
