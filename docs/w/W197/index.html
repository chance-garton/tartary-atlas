Sharaf al-Zaman Tahir Marvazi on China, the Turks and India: Arabic text (c. AD 1120) with English translation and commentary (James G. Forlong Fund XXII, London 1942)

طبائع الحیوان

Marvazi (fl. c.1120); Vladimir Minorsky (editor and translator)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier Arabic geography (Jayhani tradition) with new twelfth-century information from Seljuk court informants on the Qun-Qay migrations and the Qara Khitai

Cautions
  • Minorsky's chapter text is an extract; the full Taba'i al-hayawan remains partly unpublished.

Why it matters

Contains the famous passage on the chain-migration of the Qun, Qay, Sari and Turkmen across the steppe c.1000-1050, a primary datum for steppe ethnogenesis before the Mongols.
