Observations of a Journey in the Russian Empire in the Year 1772 (and in 1773 and 1774), 2 volumes, with the new map of Lake Baikal

Johann Gottlieb Georgi

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation: Georgi travelled with Falck then Pallas, circumnavigated Lake Baikal 1772 and crossed the Barabinsk steppe and Ural

Cautions
  • Partly overlaps with Pallas's Reise for 1772-1773 since they travelled together in stretches.

Why it matters

Detailed eyewitness ethnography of the Baikal Buryats and of the Irkutsk frontier with Qing Mongolia in the 1770s; includes the first accurate survey of Lake Baikal (separate IA map item neuecartedesbaikalseeswelchebeyumschiffu73).
