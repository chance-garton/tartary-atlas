Book of Campaigns of Islam Giray III

A Crimean Tatar chronicle of the khan's alliance with Khmelnytsky's Cossacks against Poland, 1648 to 1650.

Full title Book of Campaigns: chronicle of Islam Giray III’s campaigns with Khmelnytsky’s Cossacks against Poland (1648-1650), Russian translation, Simferopol 1998

تاريخ اسلام گرای خان (كتاب تاريخ)

Haci Mehmed Senai of Crimea (fl. 1651)

Open the original book

Haci Mehmed Senai wrote this Ottoman Turkish chronicle at Bakhchysarai in 1651. It describes the campaigns of Khan Islam Giray III in Ukraine, including the Zboriv and Zbarazh campaign, fought with Khmelnytsky's Zaporozhian Cossacks against Poland. It shows the Crimean khanate as a military power in the mid-seventeenth century. The atlas does not hold the text: the card records a 1998 Simferopol Russian translation (web transcription with maps), and nothing is stored.

Who “Tartar” means here
The Crimean Tatars of Islam Giray III's khanate. The card notes that the contemporaries Beauplan and Chevalier (W078) called them Precopian Tartars.
Why read it
The Crimean Tatar side of the 1648 to 1650 wars in Ukraine, told by a writer at the khan's court.
Watch out
It is a court panegyric, and the Russian translation derives from Abrahamowicz's Ottoman text. The atlas quotes nothing from this work.
