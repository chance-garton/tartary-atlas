Journal of the Journey through Various Provinces of the Russian Empire (German translation of Dnevnye zapiski, vols 1-2 covering 1768-1769 and 1770)

Дневные записки путешествия ... по разным провинциям Российского государства

Ivan Lepekhin

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation by the Russian academician Lepekhin on the Orenburg expedition (Volga, Ural, Bashkiria); German translation by Hase

Cautions
  • Translation; Hase's preface explains the relation to the Russian 1771 first volume. Third German volume (1783) unverified.
  • Russian original is in the Russian tradition's remit.

Why it matters

Russian academician's eyewitness journal of the Volga-Ural frontier in the same years as Pallas, available to German readers; documents Bashkir and Kalmyk settlements and the Orenburg line from a Russian participant.
