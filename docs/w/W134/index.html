Sino-Foreign Glossary (Sino-Mongolian vocabulary and documents in Chinese transcription), 1389

華夷譯語

Huo Yuanjie and Mashaikh (Hongwu court translators)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

official Ming translation office compilation of Mongolian vocabulary and sample Mongol letters, transcribed with Chinese characters

Cautions
  • Several later 'Huayi yiyu' compilations (Siyi guan vocabularies of the 15th-16th c.) circulate under the same title; confirm on the title page that this is the 1389 Hongwu glossary. Attribution of the Peking copy's edition unknown.

Why it matters

Earliest Ming record of Mongolian as spoken by the post-Yuan Mongols, with documents addressed to the Ming court; a linguistic anchor for reading Mongol names in Ming sources.
