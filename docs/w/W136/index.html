Illustrated Treatise on the Maritime Kingdoms (50 juan 1844; 60 juan 1847; 100 juan 1852), with chapters on Russia (E’luosi) and maps

海國圖志

Wei Yuan

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation from Western geographical works in Chinese translation, Chinese travel accounts and official records

Cautions
  • If the copy is the 1852 edition its composition date exceeds 1850 by two years; the Russia chapters derive largely from the 1844-47 editions and from earlier Chinese sources (Tulisen, Qi Yunshi). Record edition after checking the title page.

Why it matters

Mid-19th-century Chinese synthesis of world geography including the Russian empire in Asia; shows how Qing scholars mapped 'Tartar' regions using both Chinese and translated European sources.
