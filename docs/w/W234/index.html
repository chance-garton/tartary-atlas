China Illustrated by its Sacred and Secular Monuments (China illustrata)

Athanasius Kircher, S.J.

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of Jesuit reports (Grueber and Dorville's 1661-1664 overland journey through Tibet, Trigault/Goes on Cathay, Martini) by an author who never travelled to Asia; includes Kircher's own theories

Cautions
  • Second-hand compilation; Kircher's speculative etymologies and Egyptian-origin theories are his own; Grueber's original notes lost, surviving mainly through Kircher and Thévenot.
  • Dutch 1668 (gri_33125008480481) and French 1670 translations exist.

Why it matters

The most widely read seventeenth-century Latin synthesis on China, Tibet and Tartary; argues explicitly that Cathay is China and reproduces Grueber's route through Tartary and Tibet, thereby shaping the European atlas image of 'Tartaria Magna'.
