Journal of Two Journeys made in 1727, 1728 and 1736 from Kiakhta and Tsurukhaitu through Mongolia to Peking, with a geographical-historical description of the city of Peking

Lorenz Lange; P. S. Pallas (ed.)

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation: Lange led Russian caravans to Beijing; edited posthumously by Pallas from manuscripts

Cautions
  • Lange's earlier 1715-1717 and 1721-1722 journals were printed in Weber's Das veränderte Russland and in Bell; this 1781 volume prints the later journals. Pallas as editor may have abridged.
  • IA bub_gb_rWABAAAAQAAJ is mislabelled English in catalogue; the text is German.

Why it matters

Eyewitness Gobi crossings between the Kiakhta treaty frontier and Beijing, complementing Lange's earlier journal known in English only through Bell (W001) and Weber; describes Mongol posts, Qing frontier control and the caravan trade.
