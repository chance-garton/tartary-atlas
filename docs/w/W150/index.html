The Excellent Wish-fulfilling Tree: history of Buddhism in India, China, Tibet and Mongolia (1748), with chronological tables (re’u mig)

འཕགས་ཡུལ་རྒྱ་ནག་ཆེན་པོ་བོད་དང་སོག་ཡུལ་དུ་དམ་པའི་ཆོས་བྱུང་ཚུལ་དཔག་བསམ་ལྗོན་བཟང་།

Sumpa Khenpo Yeshe Peljor (1704-1788)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective compilation by a Mongol (Oirat-descended) Amdo scholar, with firsthand knowledge of 18th-century Kokonor and Mongol affairs

Cautions
  • Sumpa's chronology depends on Tibetan calendrical tradition (rab byung cycles) and on the Deb ther sngon po; Das's 1908 edition is partial and typographically imperfect.

Why it matters

Contains a chronological table (re'u mig) dating events year by year in Tibetan, Mongol and Chinese history, including Mongol khans: a native chronology to test against the New Chronology claims.
