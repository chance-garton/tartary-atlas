Journey through Various Provinces of the Russian Empire (Reise durch verschiedene Provinzen des Russischen Reichs), 3 parts in 5 volumes

Peter Simon Pallas

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation: Pallas's own day-by-day expedition journal 1768-1774 with informant material on Kalmyk, Bashkir, Kazakh, Buryat and Mongol peoples

Cautions
  • Pallas incorporates reports of his students and colleagues (Sokolov, Zuev, Bykov) and earlier Academy material (Müller, Gmelin); vol. 3 title on MDZ misprints the year as 1173 for 1773 (catalogue transcription of a title-page misprint; record both). A Frankfurt/Leipzig abridged edition (Ausführlicher Auszug, 1776-1778, IA 10709629bsb, 10709630bsb, 10709631bsb, 10715587bsb, 10715588bsb) exists and is not the original text.
  • Pallas also has a French translation (Voyages, Paris 1788-1793) and the IA item KarteeinesTheilsderTobollskischenundIrku65 is a separate map from vol. 3 (Tobolsk and Irkutsk governments, 1776).
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/11450096bsb; archive.org/details/11450097bsb; api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v…; api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v…; api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v…; api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v…

Why it matters

The core Academy expedition journal for the Volga-Ural-Siberia belt in 1768-1774: eyewitness description of Kalmyk hordes, Bashkirs, Kazakh steppe frontier lines, Siberian Tatars, Buryats and the Kiakhta border, with plates and maps; the German original of the material the English pass only knows through Pallas's later southern journey (W005). Resolves lead L011.
