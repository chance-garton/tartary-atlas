Written source · German · German

Collection of Russian History (Sammlung Russischer Geschichte), 9 volumes in 54 parts, St Petersburg 1732-1764; contains Müller’s Siberian history, the Nachrichten von Seereisen (1758), pieces on the Kalmyks, Samoyeds, Yugra, the Amur and Chinese-Russian relations

Gerhard Friedrich Müller (ed.)

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

mixed: retrospective history from Siberian archival documents Müller copied 1733-1743 (Sibirische Geschichte, vols 6 and 8), firsthand and informant material from the Second Kamchatka Expedition, and edited reports of others

Cautions
  • Serial with several contributors; individual pieces variously by Müller, Fischer, Steller and others; a 1777-1779 Offenbach reprint (IA 11249233bsb etc., sammlungrussisc00unkngoog) rearranges content. Russian Istoriia Sibiri (IA istoriya_sibiri_vol_1) is the later edition of Müller's Russian text.
  • The English pass has Coxe (W086) and Conquest of Siberia (1842, IA conquestsiberia00pallgoog) which derive from this serial.
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/mullergfsammlungrussischer…; archive.org/details/mullergfsammlungrussischer…; archive.org/details/mullergfsammlungrussischer…; archive.org/details/mullergfsammlungrussischer…; archive.org/details/mullergfsammlungrussischer…; archive.org/details/11249233bsb

Why it matters

Foundation of Siberian historiography: Müller's history of the conquest of the Siberian khanate from Tobolsk and other archives, the Nachrichten von Seereisen (Bering, Dezhnev), essays on Kalmyk, Samoyed and Yugra questions and on the Amur. Central for how eighteenth-century scholars reconstructed pre-Russian Siberia and Tartary from documents rather than travellers' reports.
