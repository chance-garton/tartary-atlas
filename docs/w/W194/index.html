Cautions
  • Continues and copies Mirza Haidar for the earlier part; the IA scan is a modern copyrighted reissue uploaded by a third party.

Why it matters

Only detailed native narrative for Kashgaria between Mirza Haidar and the Dzungar conquest; central for the Khoja polity of 'Little Bukharia'.
