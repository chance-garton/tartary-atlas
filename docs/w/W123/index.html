Journey through Siberia from Tobolsk to Nerchinsk and the Chinese border by the Russian envoy Nikolai Spafarii in 1675 (Zapiski RGO po otdeleniiu etnografii, vol. 10.1, 1882)

Путешествие чрез Сибирь от Тобольска до Нерчинска и границ Китая русского посланника Николая Спафария в 1675 году

Nicolae Milescu Spathary (Spafarii), Russian envoy; ed. Yurii Arsenyev

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation by the envoy, with route instructions (nakazy) and dispatches (otpiski) to Tsar Aleksei as appendices

Cautions
  • vostlit states source: Arsenyev 1882 (Zapiski RGO, otd. etnografii, X.1); Spafarii drew on Martini and Jesuit geography for the China book; the Siberian diary is firsthand. His Skazanie o velikoi reke Amure also on vostlit (Spapharij_II) from Beloborova 1982.
  • China description: RSL record 01010815864, Opisanie pervyia chasti vselennyia imenuemoi Azii, Kazan 1910, ed. Katanov/Yatsimirsky, Church Slavonic text, 271 pp.; catalogue record only, no open scan found (recorded as candidate). Romanian translations on IA: Descrierea Chinei 1975 (0156-spatarul-nicolae-milescu-descrierea-chinei-1975); Jurnal de calatorie in China.
  • Further copies: vostlit.info/Texts/rus15/Spapharij/pril2.phtml…; vostlit.info/Texts/rus15/Spapharij/karten.htm

Why it matters

Resolves L010. Detailed 1675 route description of the whole of Siberia to the Qing frontier by a learned Greek-Moldavian envoy, with the Muscovite route instructions that show what maps and knowledge the Posolskii prikaz held; the companion China description remained unpublished until 1910.
