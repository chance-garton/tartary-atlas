Records / W253
Written source · French · French and Dutch

Accounts of various curious voyages never before published or translated from Hakluyt, Purchas and other English, Dutch, Portuguese, German and Spanish travellers and from some Persian, Arabic and other oriental authors (4 parts, 1663-1672; reissued 1696)

Melchisedech Thevenot, editor

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier sources: includes Martini's Relation de la guerre des Tartares (Manchu conquest), the Abulfeda excerpts on Khwarazm and Transoxiana from Greaves' Latin, Bento de Goes, the Dutch embassies, Russian route to China (Voyage de Moscou a la Chine) and Tartar material from Purchas

Cautions
  • Entirely derivative: each piece has its own transmission (Hakluyt/Purchas translations, Greaves' Latin Abulfeda, Jesuit letters). Part contents vary between issues; a table of contents check against Camus' Memoire (1802) is needed before citing a piece by volume.
  • Pieces of Tartary interest include: Martini, Relation de la guerre des Tartares; Abulfeda, Description de la Chorasmie et du Mawaralnahr (Greaves); Voyage de Moscou a la Chine (Russian embassy route); Grueber and Dorville Tibet notice. Verify placement in the copies above before citation.
  • Further copies: gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k1165044; gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k8728860c

Why it matters

The French gateway for Tartary material between 1663 and 1700: Thevenot printed the first French Martini on the Manchu conquest, the Abulfeda geography of Transoxiana, and the Russian overland route to China, all of which Witsen, Du Halde and Deguignes reused.
