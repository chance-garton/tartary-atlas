Records / W266
Written source · French · French and Dutch

Journey into Siberia made by order of the king in 1761, containing the manners and customs of the Russians and the present state of that power, the geographical description and levelling of the route from Paris to Tobolsk (2 vols in 3 and atlas; with vol. 2 the French translation of Krasheninnikov’s Kamchatka)

Jean Chappe d'Auteroche

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation (Chappe travelled Paris-St Petersburg-Tobolsk for the 1761 transit of Venus); vol. 2 is a translation of Krasheninnikov (compilation)

Cautions
  • Vol. 2 (Kamchatka) is a translation of Krasheninnikov 1755 (Russian agent's domain, English W007). Chappe's ethnographic remarks were disputed at the time (Antidote, 1770).

Why it matters

A French academician's eyewitness of Tobolsk and the Siberian Tatar and Ugrian populations in 1761, with the first French Krasheninnikov (W007 is the English); the book provoked Catherine II's Antidote, so it also documents the politics of describing Siberia.
