Preliminary Report on a Work Prepared for the Press concerning Great Tartary and the Kingdom of Siberia, with an appendix on Great Russia

Philipp Johann von Strahlenberg

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

author's own summary of firsthand and compiled material; announces the 1730 work

Cautions
  • Prospectus for the 1730 work; content overlaps with its preface.
  • Short (48 images). Distinct imprint from the 1730 book; kept as its own record because the English pass has no equivalent.

Why it matters

Shows how Strahlenberg framed Great Tartary and Siberia in 1726 before the full work appeared; useful for tracing the genesis and intended structure of the 1730 description.
