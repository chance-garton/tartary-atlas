Records / W216
Written source · Tatar · Persian, Arabic, Ottoman, Chagatai and Tatar

Compendium of Chronicles: Tatar (Nogay-Turkic) translation of parts of Rashid al-Din with an original continuation on the Jochid khans (Urus Khan, Toqtamish, Edigu, Kazan and Kasimov rulers) dedicated to Uraz Muhammad Khan of Kasimov, 1602; manuscript copy dated 20 March 1842

جامع التواریخ (ترجمهٔ ترکی جامع رشیدی)

Qadir Ali Beg Jalayir (fl. 1602), of the Kasimov court

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation (translation of Rashid al-Din) plus original dastans on the fifteenth-sixteenth-century Jochid houses from Nogay and Kazakh oral tradition and the author's own experience at Kasimov

Cautions
  • Two-thirds is a translation of Rashid al-Din; the 1842 date is the copy date, not the composition. Catalogue language code 'nog' reflects the BnF cataloguer's identification as Nogay Tatar.
  • Berezin's 1854 edition and the Kazan 1851 lithograph (Sbornik letopisei, tatarskii tekst) are the printed leads; try rusneb.ru or Kazan Federal University digital library from another environment.

Why it matters

The earliest surviving historical work in Volga Tatar, the native record of the post-Horde khanates (Kazan, Kasimov, Nogays) that Muscovy absorbed; the text behind Berezin's Kazan 1854 print (Sbornik letopisei, Biblioteka vostochnykh istorikov II, 1), which was not found online.
