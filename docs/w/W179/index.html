Description of Dzungaria and Eastern Turkestan in ancient and present condition (2 parts, 1829)

Описание Чжуньгарии и Восточного Туркистана в древнем и нынешнем состоянии

Bichurin, translator; from Xiyu wenjian lu and Qing gazetteers

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation from Qing sources (Xiyu tuzhi, Xiyu wenjian lu) with translator notes; not eyewitness

Cautions
  • Translation with selective abridgement; Qing sources themselves compiled after the 1759 conquest.

Why it matters

The Qing administrative description of the region Europeans called Chinese Tartary or Little Bukharia, in Russian, decades before Valikhanov; gives Qing names and distances for the Tarim oases.
