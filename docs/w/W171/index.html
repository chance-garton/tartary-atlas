Official embassy report (stateinyi spisok) of the Baikov mission to China, 1654-1657

Статейный список посольства Ф. И. Байкова в Китай (1654-1657)

Fyodor Baikov, Russian envoy

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand official record of route Tobolsk, Irtysh, Dzungar (Kalmyk) camps, Gobi, Beijing and Qing court protocol dispute

Cautions
  • Two published texts (1788 Novikov from a manuscript copy; 1966 critical edition from archival original); both on vostlit with source statements.

Why it matters

Earliest Russian official account of crossing Dzungar territory and of Qing Beijing; describes the Kalmyk taishas and Bukharan trade of the Irtysh in the 1650s.
