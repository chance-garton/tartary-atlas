Book of Ubaydullah Khan (Ubaydulla-name)

A court chronicle of Bukhara under Ubaydullah Khan II, 1702 to 1711.

Full title Book of Ubaydullah Khan: contemporary history of Bukhara 1702-1711, Russian translation by A. A. Semenov, Tashkent 1957

عبیدالله نامه

Mir Muhammad Amin Bukhari (fl. 1700-1716)

Open the original book

Mir Muhammad Amin Bukhari, an official of Ubaydullah Khan II (reigned 1702 to 1711), wrote this contemporary Persian chronicle of Bukhara around 1711 to 1716. It is detailed on tribal amirs, Kazakh raids and the politics of Bukhara, Samarkand and Balkh, at the time European maps were fixing Bucharia and Independent Tartary. The atlas does not hold the text: only A. A. Semenov's 1957 Russian translation, as a web transcription, is recorded, and nothing is stored.

Watch out
It is a panegyric written to praise the khan, and no critical Persian edition exists. The atlas quotes nothing from this work.
