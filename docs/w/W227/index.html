History of the Bishops of Salona and Split, chapters 36-40 on the Tartars (first printed edition, in Lucius, On the Kingdom of Dalmatia and Croatia)

Thomas, archdeacon of Split; Ivan Lučić (Giovanni Lucio), editor

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation for 1241-1242: Thomas witnessed King Béla IV's flight to Split and Trogir and the Mongol pursuit along the Dalmatian coast; includes an ethnographic chapter 'De natura Tartarorum'

Cautions
  • Thomas relies partly on hearsay for the battle of Mohi; the 'De natura Tartarorum' chapter mixes observation with topoi. Modern editions: Rački 1894 (Monumenta spectantia historiam Slavorum meridionalium 26, not located on IA among labelled volumes) and Karbić/Perić/Sweeney CEMT 4 (2006).

Why it matters

Independent Dalmatian eyewitness of the 1241-1242 Mongol invasion, with a description of Tartar appearance, weapons and tactics, and of the pursuit of the Hungarian king to the Adriatic; complements Rogerius.
