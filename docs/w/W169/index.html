Written source · Russian · Russian and Siberian

Short Siberian (Kungur) Chronicle with 154 drawings (photolithographic facsimile of the Academy of Sciences manuscript, 1880)

Краткая сибирская летопись (Кунгурская) со 154 рисунками

Semyon Remezov (attributed); preface A. Zost, Archaeographic Commission

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective illustrated history of Yermak (c. 1700) using Esipov and Tobolsk tradition plus the Kungur chronicle of Cossack oral origin

Cautions
  • Attribution to Remezov by Miller on the basis of a colophon note; Kungur articles are a separate interpolated source; images are late 17th century imaginative reconstructions, not eyewitness.

Why it matters

Only illustrated Siberian chronicle; its 154 drawings depict Tatar towns, Kuchum and Yermak, frequently reproduced in alternative-history media without their textual context.
