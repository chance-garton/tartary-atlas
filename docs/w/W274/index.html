Newly Enlarged Description of his Great Chinese Journey, made in 1692 from Moscow via Great Ustyug, Siberia, Dauria and through Great Tartary to China and back within three years, with a preface by Paul Jacob Marperger

Adam Brand

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation: Brand travelled as secretary of the Russian embassy of E. Ysbrants Ides to Beijing 1692-1695

Cautions
  • 1698 first edition (Hamburg) and 1699 French/English versions exist (IA ned-kbn-all-00001456-001 French 1699; BrandAJournalFromTheMajesties English 1698); the 1734 edition adds Marperger's preface and appendices. Brand was an eyewitness but his text is far shorter than Ides.
  • 1698 Hamburg first edition not yet located as a scan; see candidates.

Why it matters

German eyewitness companion to Ides (W006) on the Moscow-Beijing overland route; title expressly routes the journey durch die grosse Tartarey, and the text names the Siberian and Transbaikal peoples encountered. Independent of Ides's own narrative (published later in Dutch).
