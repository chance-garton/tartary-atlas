Written source · Russian · Russian and Siberian

Journals of the 1715-1716 journey to Beijing (Russian translation, Shafranovskaya 1961) and Daily record of residence at the Chinese court in 1721 (Severnyi arkhiv 1822)

Дневники путешествий в Пекин 1715-1716 гг.; Поденная записка пребывания при китайском дворе 1721 года

Lorenz Lange, Russian agent in Beijing

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation by a resident agent; Siberian and Mongolian notes drawn from his own journeys 1715-1737

Cautions
  • Russian texts are translations from German publications (Weber 1721, Pallas Neue Nordische Beyträge); 1822 translator unnamed.

Why it matters

Resident agent testimony on Qing-Russian trade, Mongol frontier and caravan routes in the 1710s-1720s, the Russian side of the Tulishen (W016) period.
