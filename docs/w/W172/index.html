Lorenz Lange's journals from Siberia to Beijing

A Russian agent's journals of a Beijing journey and a stay at the Chinese court, 1715 to 1722.

Full title Journals of the 1715-1716 journey to Beijing (Russian translation, Shafranovskaya 1961) and Daily record of residence at the Chinese court in 1721 (Severnyi arkhiv 1822)

Дневники путешествий в Пекин 1715-1716 гг.; Поденная записка пребывания при китайском дворе 1721 года

Lorenz Lange, Russian agent in Beijing

Open the original book

Lorenz Lange served as a Russian agent in Beijing and left a journal of his 1715 to 1716 journey there and a daily record of living at the Qing court in 1721 to 1722. They cover Siberia, the Selenga, the Urga route through Mongolia and Beijing, with notes on Qing-Russian trade and caravan routes. The atlas does not hold the text: the corpus knows only Russian web texts of 1961, 1969 and 1822, all translated from German publications, and none is stored.
