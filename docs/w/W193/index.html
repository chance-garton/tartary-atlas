History of Kashgar (anonymous)

An anonymous Persian chronicle of the Moghul khans and Khojas of Kashgaria, written after 1696.

Catalogue title History of Kashgar: anonymous Persian chronicle of the Moghul khans and the Khojas of Kashgaria, Russian extracts in MIKK 1969 and Materialy po istorii kirgizov i Kirgizii 1973

تاریخ کاشغر

Anonymous (early eighteenth century)

Open the original book

This anonymous Persian chronicle, written in Kashgaria in the early eighteenth century, runs from the Mongol period to about 1700. It tells of the Moghul khans, the Naqshbandi Khojas and the Dzungar (Qalmaq) overlordship of the 1680s and 1690s. European geography called this region Kashgar or Little Bukharia within Chinese Tartary. The atlas does not hold the text: the corpus knows only Russian extracts printed in 1969 and 1973, as web transcriptions, and no Persian text or facsimile was found online.
