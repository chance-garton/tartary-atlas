Written source · German · German

New Northern Contributions to Physical and Geographical Description of Lands and Peoples, Natural History and Economy, 7 volumes, 1781-1796 (continued as Neueste nordische Beyträge)

Peter Simon Pallas (ed.)

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

mixed: edited firsthand journals and reports by Russian officers, naturalists, missionaries and merchants (Lepekhin, Sujew, Rytschkow, Sokolov, Sievers's Altai and Zunghar letters, Kamchatka and Aleutian voyages, Kalmyk pieces), plus Pallas's own essays

Cautions
  • Serial: authorship differs per article; several pieces are translations from Russian official reports. Catalogue volume numbering differs between the RSL, CIHM and Google copies.
  • Predecessor serial Nordische Beyträge (Büsching) not searched.
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/cihm_38273; archive.org/details/cihm_38276; archive.org/details/cihm_38277; archive.org/details/neuenordischebe02beitgoog

Why it matters

The principal German-language outlet for Siberian, Altai, Central Asian and Pacific field reports between Pallas's Reise and the 1800s; contains Johann Sievers's letters from the Altai and the Zunghar frontier (1790s), documents on the Kirgis-Kaisak (Kazakh) hordes and Kalmyks, and Pallas's commentary. Must be indexed piece by piece.
