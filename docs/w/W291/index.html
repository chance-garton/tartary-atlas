Written source · German · German

Asia Polyglotta (Paris 1823; 2nd ed. 1831), with the Sprachatlas: comparative vocabularies and ethnogenealogical classification of the peoples of Asia (Mongols, Tungus, Turks, Samoyeds, Finns, Caucasians, Tibetans and others)

Julius von Klaproth

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of vocabularies from earlier collectors (Pallas, Strahlenberg, Messerschmidt, Fischer, the 1787 Vocabularia comparativa) and from Klaproth's own fieldwork on the Siberian-Mongol frontier 1805-1806 and in the Caucasus 1807-1808, with historical extracts from Chinese and other Asian sources

Cautions
  • Vocabularies are second-hand for most languages; Klaproth's Chinese historical extracts are his own translations and have been criticized. Paris imprint by a German author writing in German (eligible as German-language original).

Why it matters

Klaproth's classification replaced the loose Tartar category with linguistically defined Turkic, Mongol and Tungus families and traced the historical Tatars through Chinese sources; a key document for how the term Tartary was dismantled in nineteenth-century scholarship.
