Book of the Great Map, or Ancient Map of the Russian State, renewed in the Razriad and written into a book in 1627 (2nd edition, 1838)

Книга Большому чертежу, или Древняя карта Российского государства, поновленная в Разряде и списанная в книгу 1627 года

Razriad (Military Chancellery) of Muscovy; edition by D. I. Yazykov

How we know

official or contemporary record An official document or a record made at the time.

Card wording

official record: verbal description of the lost Bolshoi Chertezh (c. 1600) and its 1627 renewal, compiled from service route-books (stateinye and dorozhniki); compilation not travel

Cautions
  • Text describes a map that is lost; several manuscript redactions; OCR of the imprint reads 1858 while catalogue and bibliographies give 1838 (both recorded). 1950 Serbina edition not located on IA (see candidate).
  • | Also recorded independently by the maps noneuropean pass: Item page retrieved (HTTP 200) with full bibliographic description; viewer not tested from this environment (prlib.ru/item/417685).
  • Further copies: rusneb (not reachable from here) rusneb.ru/catalog/000199_000009_003542890/

Why it matters

The most detailed Russian geographical text of the early 17th century, listing rivers, towns and nomad camps from the Dnieper to the Ob, with the Bukhara and Siberian roads; central for what Muscovy actually knew of the steppe and Tartary before Witsen and Remezov.
