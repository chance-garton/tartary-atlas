Card wording

firsthand observation 1768-1775 (Don, Volga, Astrakhan, Terek, Kabarda, Ossetia, Georgia, Kuban, Crimea approaches); Pallas edited the incomplete manuscripts

Cautions
  • Posthumous, edited by Pallas; Klaproth's 1815 re-edition Reisen nach Georgien und Imerethi (IA 10430666bsb) rearranges the material.

Why it matters

Foundational Academy survey of the Caucasus and the Don-Volga-Terek steppe, with Kalmyk and Nogai observations and the first comparative Caucasian vocabularies (later exploited by Klaproth, who was accused of plagiarizing it).
