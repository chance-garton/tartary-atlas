Written source · French · French and Dutch

Historical journal of the voyage of M. de Lesseps, French consul attached to La Perouse’s expedition, from his leaving the frigates at Petropavlovsk in Kamchatka until his arrival in France (overland across Siberia 1787-1788), 2 vols

Jean-Baptiste-Barthelemy de Lesseps

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation (Lesseps crossed Kamchatka, Okhotsk, Yakutsk, Irkutsk and Siberia by dog-sled and horse)

Cautions
  • One IA copy (journalhistoriq37unkngoog) is dated 1788 in metadata although the book appeared in 1790: catalogue conflict recorded.

Why it matters

French eyewitness of the far north-east of Siberia between Krasheninnikov and Sauer (W008), describing Koryak, Yakut and Tungus communities under Russian rule; a useful check on the English-language Siberian narratives.
