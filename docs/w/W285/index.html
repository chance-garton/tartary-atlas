Cautions
  • IA catalogue title misreads Steller as Weller; Pallas edited and annotated the manuscript.

Why it matters

Marks the eastern terminus of the Siberian source field: eyewitness voyage from Kamchatka to America; useful as a control on eighteenth-century knowledge of the Chukchi and the northeastern limit of Asia.
