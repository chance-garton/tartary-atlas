Records / W198
Written source · English · Persian, Arabic, Ottoman, Chagatai and Tatar

Delight of Hearts, geographical part: G. Le Strange’s English translation (Gibb Memorial XXIII, 2, 1919) and a seventeenth-century Persian manuscript (BULAC MS.PERS.84)

نزهة القلوب

Hamdallah Mustawfi of Qazvin (d. after 1340)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier geographers with the author's Ilkhanid fiscal knowledge (he was a financial official); c.1340

Cautions
  • Mustawfi copies Yaqut and earlier sources for regions outside Iran; the BULAC catalogue tags the manuscript language 'Arabic' in error (the text is Persian).

Why it matters

Ilkhanid-era administrative geography of the Mongol domains in Iran with the standard Persian scheme of climes and routes; Le Strange's Persian text (GMS XXIII, 1, 1915) is the companion still to be located.
