Muscovy (report of the papal legation to Ivan IV, with the Commentarii on Muscovy and the Tatars)

Antonio Possevino, S.J.

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation (papal legate at Moscow and Staritsa 1581-1582, mediator of the Jam Zapolski truce) plus compilation from Herberstein and Muscovite informants

Cautions
  • Uses Herberstein; polemical Counter-Reformation purpose. English translation Graham 1977 (lending only).

Why it matters

Jesuit eyewitness assessment of Muscovy immediately after the conquest of Kazan and Astrakhan and during the Livonian war; describes Tatar dependencies of the tsar and the routes east.
