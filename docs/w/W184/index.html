Book of Victory of the Kazan Province (Zafer-name-i vilayet-i Kazan, 1550), Russian translation by F. Khakimzyanov, Ekho vekov 1995 no. 1

Зафер наме-и вилает-и Казан

Sherifi Hajji Tarkhani

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand: author present in Kazan during the 1550 siege by Ivan IV, writing shortly after for an Ottoman audience

Cautions
  • Single Istanbul manuscript; translation from a Turkish edition; authorship of Sherifi accepted by editors.

Why it matters

The only known Kazan-side contemporary account of the Muscovite assault, a native counter-witness to the Kazan Chronicle and Nikon chronicle.
