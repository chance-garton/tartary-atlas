Written source · French · French and Dutch

Journey in Armenia and Persia in 1805 and 1806, followed by a notice on Gilan and Mazandaran by Colonel Trezel (with observations on the Turkmen and Kurds)

Pierre-Amedee Jaubert

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation (Jaubert carried Napoleon's letters to Fath Ali Shah; imprisoned at Bayazid; describes Turkmen and Kurdish tribes en route); Trezel's notice from the Gardane mission

Cautions
  • Turkmen material largely reported, not observed; Trezel's notice is a separate author's work.
  • Lower priority; included because the task list named it.

Why it matters

Peripheral to Tartary proper: offers a French diplomat's notes on the Turkmen of the Caspian shore and the Qajar frontier, useful for the southern edge of "Independent Tartary" on French maps of the 1820s. Jaubert also edited Meyendorff (recorded separately).
