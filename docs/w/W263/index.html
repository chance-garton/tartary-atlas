Written source · French · French and Dutch

Historical tables of Asia from the monarchy of Cyrus to our days, with historical and ethnographic researches on that part of the world (text volume; atlas of 27 maps separate)

Julius Klaproth

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier sources (Chinese, Persian, Greek, Russian) with Klaproth's own Caucasus and Siberian frontier observations of 1805-1808 informing the ethnography

Cautions
  • Klaproth's racial-linguistic identifications (e.g. Massagetae as Germanic) are his own conjectures; treat as retrospective interpretation. The accompanying atlas (27 maps) is a separate physical item.
  • German-language Klaproth works (Reise in den Kaukasus, Asia polyglotta) belong to the German agent; only the French items recorded here.

Why it matters

Klaproth's attempt to map the succession of peoples over Inner Asia period by period; the commentary states which ancient and medieval names he equates with which modern Tartar peoples and why, making the reasoning behind such identifications inspectable.
