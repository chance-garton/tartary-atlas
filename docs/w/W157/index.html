How we know

Card wording

official record: assembly laws of the Khalkha aimaks under Qing suzerainty, from Mongolian manuscripts

Cautions
  • Edition from Leningrad manuscripts; Mongolian text given in transcription, not facsimile. Copyright of the 1965 edition applies to the upload.

Why it matters

Native legal record of Khalkha society under Qing rule, naming banners, lamaseries and offices: the internal organisation of the polity Europeans called Mongol Tartary.
