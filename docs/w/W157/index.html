The Khalkha Jirum, laws of the Khalkha Mongols

The assembly laws of the Khalkha Mongols under Qing rule, 1709 to 1770, in a 1965 Soviet edition.

Catalogue title Khalkha Jirum: monument of Mongolian law of the 18th century (Mongolian text in transcription with Russian translation)

Халха Джирум. Памятник монгольского феодального права XVIII в.

Khalkha Jirum (laws of the Khalkha, 1709-1770); edited by Zhamtsarano and Dylykov (Moscow 1965)

The Khalkha Jirum is a code of laws of the Khalkha Mongols of Outer Mongolia, first issued in 1709 with additions to 1770, when the Khalkha were under Qing suzerainty. It names banners, monasteries and offices and shows how the people Europeans called Mongol Tartars organised themselves. The card lists the 1965 Moscow edition by Zhamtsarano and Dylykov, the Mongolian text in transcription with a Russian translation, from a user-uploaded scan. The atlas does not hold the text of this edition: the card says copyright of the 1965 edition applies to the upload, and the corpus stores no text of it, so nothing was read for this guide.
