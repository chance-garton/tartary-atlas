Imperially Commissioned Researches on the Origins of the Manchus, 20 juan (1777-1783)

欽定滿洲源流考

Qianlong commission (Agui, Yu Minzhong)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

official compilation reinterpreting Chinese historical records (Sushen, Mohe, Bohai, Jurchen) as Manchu ancestry

Cautions
  • Ideologically driven compilation with systematic re-transcription of names; Siku quanshu (1770s-80s) manuscript copy: the Qianlong editors systematically re-transcribed Mongol, Jurchen and Khitan names following the Qinding Liao Jin Yuan san shi guoyu jie, so name forms differ from Ming and early Qing prints (seen on the Ming shi Wala chapter page: 衛拉特 for 瓦剌, 太平巴圖博囉 for 太平 and 把禿孛羅). Cite the pre-Siku editions for original orthography.

Why it matters

Qing court's own construction of Manchu origins and of the historical geography of Manchuria, including the etymologies of place names; a state-produced 'origins' narrative to compare with European ethnographies of the Tartars.
