Journey through the Altai Mountains and the Zungharian Kirghiz Steppe, undertaken in 1826 at the expense of the Imperial University of Dorpat, 2 parts with plates and maps (Berlin 1829-1830)

Carl Friedrich von Ledebour, with C. A. Meyer and A. von Bunge

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation 1826 in the Russian Altai, the Bukhtarma and Irtysh and the Kazakh (Kirghiz) steppe on the Qing frontier; part 2 contains Meyer's and Bunge's separate journals

Cautions
  • Three authors; part 2 journals by Meyer and Bunge are separately attributable. Multiple duplicated Google copies with inconsistent volume tagging.

Why it matters

Eyewitness account of the Altai frontier zone between the Russian mining district and the former Zunghar lands, including the Ablaikit ruins and Kazakh camps; part of the 1820s wave that replaced generalized Tartary with surveyed Kirghiz steppe.
