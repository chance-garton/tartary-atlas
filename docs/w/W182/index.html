Journal of the interpreter M. Tevkelev, sent to the Junior Horde to negotiate the submission of the Kazakhs, 3 Oct 1731 to 14 Jan 1733 (in Kazakhsko-russkie otnosheniia v XVI-XVIII vekakh, Alma-Ata 1961, doc. no. 33)

Журнал переводчика М. Тевкелева, ездившего в Малый жуз для переговоров о подданстве казахов (1731-1733)

Mamet Tevkelev (Kutlu-Muhammad Tevkelev), Tatar interpreter of the College of Foreign Affairs

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand diplomatic journal by a Muslim Tatar official among the Kazakhs of Abulkhair Khan; surrounding documents are official correspondence

Cautions
  • Published in a Soviet documentary collection with abridgement (Iz zhurnala); Tevkelev also wrote later memoranda used by Rychkov and Levshin.

Why it matters

Eyewitness record of the 1731 Kazakh submission negotiations, the constitutional moment for Russian rule in the steppe; whole collection (1594-1770) gives Russian archival view of Kazakh and Dzungar politics.
