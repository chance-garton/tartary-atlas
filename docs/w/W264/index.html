Written source · French · French and Dutch

Memoirs relating to Asia, containing historical, geographical and philological researches on the peoples of the East (3 vols, 1824-1828)

Julius Klaproth

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

mixed: translations of Chinese and Manchu texts on Central Asia (e.g. on the Uighurs, Kirghiz, Khotan, the Amur), critiques of contemporaries, and some material from Klaproth's 1805-1806 Siberian frontier journey

Cautions
  • Volume numbering in IA metadata conflicts with imprint years (mmoiresrelatifs01klapgoog is dated 1826 and labelled vol. 2); verify against title pages before citing.
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/dli.pahar.0087

Why it matters

Contains Klaproth's French translations from Chinese sources on the Uighurs, on the geography of Central Asia and on the Amur, plus his critique of Remusat; a key channel by which Chinese descriptions of "Tartary" entered French scholarship.
