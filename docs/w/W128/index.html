The Mongol-Oirat Laws of 1640

The law code agreed by Mongol and Oirat princes in 1640, with later additions, edited in Russian in 1880.

Catalogue title The Mongol-Oirat Laws of 1640, the supplementary decrees of Galdan Khung Taiji, and the laws compiled for the Volga Kalmyks under Donduk Dashi (St Petersburg 1880)

Монголо-ойратские законы 1640 года, дополнительные указы Галдан-Хун-Тайджия и законы, составленные для волжских калмыков при калмыцком хане Дондук-Даши

Konstantin Golstunsky (editor and translator)

Open the original book

In 1640 a Khalkha and Oirat assembly adopted a written code of laws for the steppe peoples. Later supplements were made under Galdan in the 1670s and 1680s and, for the Volga Kalmyks, under Donduk Dashi in the 1740s. Konstantin Golstunsky published the Oirat clear-script text with a Russian translation in St Petersburg in 1880, working from Kalmyk manuscripts. The atlas holds a stored machine reading (OCR) of this scan, of good quality, but a keyword search found no passages mentioning Tartars, so nothing was read for this guide.

Not recorded. The card names the Khalkha Mongols, the Oirats (Dzungars), the Volga Kalmyks and the Russian state.
Why read it
It is a native legal text of the steppe polities of the seventeenth century, showing how Mongol and Oirat rulers described their own order, independent of European Tartary framing.
Watch out
Golstunsky's text comes from Kalmyk manuscripts, and the card says a fuller critical edition by Dylykov (1981) was not found open.
