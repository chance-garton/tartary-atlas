Written source · Oirat · Chinese, Manchu, Mongolian, Tibetan (texts)

The Mongol-Oirat Laws of 1640, the supplementary decrees of Galdan Khung Taiji, and the laws compiled for the Volga Kalmyks under Donduk Dashi (St Petersburg 1880)

Монголо-ойратские законы 1640 года, дополнительные указы Галдан-Хун-Тайджия и законы, составленные для волжских калмыков при калмыцком хане Дондук-Даши

Konstantin Golstunsky (editor and translator)

How we know

official or contemporary record An official document or a record made at the time.

Card wording

official record: text of the 1640 code adopted by the Khalkha and Oirat assembly, with later Dzungar and Kalmyk supplements; Kalmyk (Oirat clear script) text with Russian translation

Cautions
  • Golstunsky's text is from Kalmyk manuscripts; Dylykov 1981 gives a fuller critical edition (not located open).

Why it matters

Native legal text of the steppe polities of the 17th century: shows the political self-description of Mongol and Oirat rulers and the Kalmyk-Russian relationship, independent of European 'Tartary' framing.
