Tiesenhausen's collection of sources on the Golden Horde

The standard anthology of Arabic and Persian extracts on the Golden Horde, 1884 and 1941.

Catalogue title Collection of Materials relating to the History of the Golden Horde: vol. I extracts from Arabic authors (St Petersburg 1884, Arabic texts with Russian translation); vol. II extracts from Persian authors (Moscow-Leningrad 1941)

Сборник материалов, относящихся к истории Золотой Орды

Vladimir Tiesenhausen (compiler)

Vladimir Tiesenhausen's collection gathers extracts from twenty-four Arabic authors (volume I, St Petersburg 1884, Arabic texts with Russian translation) and from Persian authors (volume II, Moscow and Leningrad 1941). They describe the Golden Horde from Batu to Toqtamish and Edigu. Writers include Ibn al-Athir, Nuwayri, Ibn Battuta, Ibn Khaldun, Juvaini and Rashid al-Din, and it prints Mamluk-Jochid letters. The atlas does not hold the text: the card lists only web transcriptions of the Russian translations, and the printed volumes were not found scanned.

Why it matters

The standard collection through which the Arabic and Persian testimony on the Golden Horde (the polity behind 'Tartaria' on European maps) has been read since 1884; vol. I also prints the Mamluk-Jochid diplomatic correspondence from Ibn Abd al-Zahir, Nuwayri and Maqrizi.
