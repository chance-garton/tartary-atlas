Imperially Commissioned Tables and Biographies of the Princes and Nobles of the Outer Mongols and Muslim Regions, 120 juan (1779-1795)

欽定外藩蒙古回部王公表傳

Qianlong commission (Qi Yunshi and others)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

official compilation from banner archives and Lifanyuan records

Cautions
  • Official and retrospective; Mongolian and Manchu versions carry the original name forms.

Why it matters

Genealogies of every Mongol and Oirat noble house under the Qing, with dates of submission: the administrative skeleton of steppe polities in the 17th-18th centuries.
