History of Buddhism in the Great Land of the Hor (Mongols): the White Lotus Garland (1819)

ཆེན་པོ་ཧོར་གྱི་ཡུལ་དུ་དམ་པའི་ཆོས་ཇི་ལྟར་དར་བའི་ཚུལ་གསལ་བར་བརྗོད་པ་པདྨ་དཀར་པོའི་ཕྲེང་བ།

Jigme Namkha (Mongol lama, fl. 1818)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective history compiled from Mongolian and Tibetan sources by a Mongol author; includes lists of Mongol khans and the Dzungar-Qing wars

Cautions
  • Compiled from Sagang Sechen and Tibetan histories; Huth's translation has known errors (see Vostrikov). BDRC W1KG15408 is a different, 20th-century Hor chos 'byung by Blo bzang rta mgrin (out of scope).

Why it matters

The standard Mongol-authored history of Mongolia in Tibetan; gives Mongol dynastic succession and the names of Mongol groups as understood in Mongolia itself c.1819.
