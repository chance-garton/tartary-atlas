Written source · Old East Slavic / Church Slavonic · Russian and Siberian

Laurentian Chronicle (Complete Collection of Russian Chronicles, vol. 1), including the Primary Chronicle and Suzdal continuation to 1305

Лаврентьевская летопись (Полное собрание русских летописей, т. 1)

Anonymous Rus chroniclers; monk Lavrentii (copyist, 1377); Archaeographic Commission edition

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier annals with contemporary entries; Mongol invasion (1237-1240), Batu, Golden Horde tribute and princely visits to the Horde recorded by near-contemporary annalists; retrospective for earlier centuries

Cautions
  • Composite: Primary Chronicle (to 1110s) plus Vladimir-Suzdal and Tver annals; 1377 copy has lacunae (898-922, 1263-1283, 1288-1294) filled by editors from Radziwill and Academy copies; IA item is a user upload, provenance of scan not stated.
  • 1846 edition (IA polnoesobranierusskihletopisejizdannoep10, 300 images, open, OCR) also includes the lost Trinity Chronicle reconstruction fragments.

Why it matters

Earliest surviving Rus chronicle codex; primary Russian-side record of the Mongol conquest, the Tatar census, tribute and Horde politics for 1223-1305, the base text against which New Chronology redatings are argued.
