Genealogy of the Turkmen: Russian translation with critical Chagatai text by A. N. Kononov (Moscow-Leningrad 1958)

شجرهٔ تراکمه

Abu'l-Ghazi Bahadur Khan

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of Oghuz legend (from Rashid al-Din's Oghuz-nama) with Turkmen oral tradition collected by the khan from tribal elders in 1659-1661; informant testimony

Cautions
  • Oghuz legend copied from Rashid al-Din; tribal lists reflect seventeenth-century informants.

Why it matters

The other work of the Khivan khan-historian, distinct from W038; it records Turkmen tribal self-description at the moment Europeans were mapping the eastern Caspian as 'Tartary'.
