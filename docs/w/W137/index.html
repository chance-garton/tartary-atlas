Record of the Waterways of the Western Regions, 5 juan (compiled during exile in Ili 1812-1819; printed 1823)

西域水道記

Xu Song (1781-1848)

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand survey and informant testimony gathered by an exiled official in Xinjiang, combined with textual research

Cautions
  • Uses the Xiyu tuzhi and Xiyu tongwen zhi for older names; Xu Song's own observations are marked in the text.

Why it matters

Most precise pre-1850 Chinese hydrographic description of Xinjiang, giving Turki, Mongol and Chinese toponyms for rivers and lakes, with maps.
