Antiquities of Indian Tibet, Part II: The Chronicles of Ladakh and Minor Chronicles (Tibetan texts and English translations, Calcutta 1926)

ལ་དྭགས་རྒྱལ་རབས།

Ladakh royal chronicle (compiled to the 17th century, continued to the 1840s); edited and translated by A. H. Francke

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective royal genealogy with contemporary sections; the minor chronicles include local records of the Mongol-Tibetan invasion of 1679-1684 and the Dogra conquest (1834-1842)

Cautions
  • Francke's text is a composite of several manuscripts (A, B, C, S); dating of early sections is legendary. Later editions: Petech 1977 (Kingdom of Ladakh).

Why it matters

Native chronicle of the western Tibetan borderland covered by Moorcroft (W019) and Vigne (W029); documents the 17th-century Mongol (Khoshut) campaign in Ladakh from the Ladakhi side.
