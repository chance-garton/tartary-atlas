An Account of Tibet (English abridged translation of Desideri’s Italian Relazione)

Ippolito Desideri; Filippo De Filippi, editor

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation (Desideri crossed Ladakh and western Tibet to Lhasa 1715-1716 and lived there until 1721, witnessing the Dzungar invasion of 1717)

Cautions
  • De Filippi abridges and rearranges Desideri; Hosten 1938 (IA dli.pahar.2700) prints letters. The Italian text is the authoritative form.
  • Italian original still a gap; see candidates.
  • Coordinating pilot: further copies located by IA search (1937 Filippi edition vols 1-2 as community upload; DLI 2015.49886) and Gallica SRU (bpt6k324709r, 1937; the ark viewer returned HTTP 500 to the Latin-Italian agent, a technical failure).
  • Further copies: archive.org/details/an-account-of-tibet-travel…; gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k324709r; archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.49886

Why it matters

Resolves the access problem of L003: open copies of the English Desideri exist. Italian original remains to be located (Puini, Il Tibet, 1904; Petech, I missionari italiani nel Tibet e nel Nepal V-VII, 1954-56): neither found on IA, Gallica or Europeana this pass.
