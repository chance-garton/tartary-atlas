Description of the Siberian Tsardom and of all that happened in it from the beginning, especially from its subjection to the Russian power (book 1; 1750, accessed 1787 reissue)

Описание Сибирского царства и всех происшедших в нем дел от начала, а особливо от покорения его Российской державе по сии времена

Gerhard Friedrich Müller

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective history compiled from Siberian archives, chronicles and Tatar informants gathered on the 1733-1743 Academy expedition

Cautions
  • Catalogue date 1787 is a reissue of the 1750 edition; Fischer (below) abridged Miller.

Why it matters

Foundational scholarly history of Siberia, first to use the Siberian chronicles and Tatar traditions critically; describes Kuchum khanate and its Nogai and Bukharan ties.
