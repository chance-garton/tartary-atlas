Chronicle of Tibet: Song of the Queen of Spring (1643)

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective history with contemporary sections on the Mongol (Khoshut) intervention of 1637-1642 that the author witnessed

Cautions
  • Modern Serthar edition without apparatus; older Lhasa block prints exist. Tucci's Tibetan Painted Scrolls vol. 2 (IA dli.pahar.2915) gives excerpts in translation.

Why it matters

Tibetan view of the Mongol-Tibetan relationship from the Yuan to the Khoshut conquest, written by the ruler who allied with Gushri Khan; English translations by Tucci (partial, 1949) and Ahmad (1995).
