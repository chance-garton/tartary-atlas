Records / W204
Written source · Arabic · Persian, Arabic, Ottoman, Chagatai and Tatar

Survey of the Countries: Arabic text edited from Paris and Leiden manuscripts (Paris 1840) and French translation with Reinaud’s general introduction on Arabic geography (Paris 1848)

تقويم البلدان

Abu'l-Fida (1273-1331), prince of Hama

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier geographers (Ibn Sa'id, Idrisi, Ibn Hawqal) with coordinates; fourteenth-century Syrian view, not firsthand for the steppe

Cautions
  • Coordinates copied from earlier tables; Reinaud's transliterations reflect nineteenth-century French practice.

Why it matters

Fourteenth-century Islamic gazetteer with coordinates for Golden Horde and Central Asian towns; Reinaud's 1848 introduction is the foundational European survey of Arabic geographical knowledge of Tartary.
