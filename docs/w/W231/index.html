Description of European Sarmatia, including Muscovy and part of Tartary (1581 Speyer edition; first edition Kraków 1578)

Alessandro Guagnini of Verona

How we know

claimed or disputed Authorship, date or authenticity is claimed or contested.

Card wording

mixed: compilation from Herberstein, Miechowita, Kromer and Polish chronicles, with firsthand observation from Guagnini's service as a military commander at Vitebsk on the Muscovite-Tatar frontier; authorship disputed (Maciej Stryjkowski claimed the work)

Cautions
  • Plagiarism dispute with Stryjkowski documented in 1580; heavy borrowing from Herberstein and Miechowita. Later Polish editions (1611, 1768 in Zbiór dziejopisów) differ.
  • First edition Kraków 1578 located at the Lower Silesian Digital Library (edition 5021, imprint Wirzbięta 1578 confirmed in catalogue metadata; viewer not tested).
  • Further copies: api.digitale-sammlungen.de/iiif/presentation/v… (MDZ 1581 copy, 254 canvases)

Why it matters

Late-sixteenth-century Latin synthesis of the Tatar hordes bordering Poland-Lithuania and Muscovy, with a section 'Tartariae descriptio'; widely reprinted (Polish translation 1611) and a major channel for the European image of Tartary.
