Records / W222
Written source · Ottoman Turkish · Persian, Arabic, Ottoman, Chagatai and Tatar

Four Ottoman documents on Crimean affairs, 1763-1780: a memoir on Russia since 1763 (Beyan-name), a memoir on Russian exactions (1773), the Russo-Crimean treaty text on frontier changes (Ahd-name, Turkish version by the reis ul-kuttab), and a memoir on the Tatars and their khans

بیان نامه؛ عهدنامه (مجموعه چهار قطعه در باب امور قریم)

Anonymous Ottoman chancery copyist; Abd al-Razzaq Efendi

How we know

official or contemporary record An official document or a record made at the time.

Card wording

official record: chancery translations and memoranda of the 1770s on the detachment of the Crimean khanate from the Ottoman empire; copy possibly by a European hand

Cautions
  • Items II-IV are translations of European or Russian texts, so the 'Ottoman' voice is partly mediated; copyist unidentified.

Why it matters

Ottoman documentary view of the end of Crimean 'Tartary' as an Ottoman dependency (Kucuk Kaynarca 1774 and after), including a native memoir on the Tatars and their khans.
