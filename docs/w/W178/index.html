History of the first four khans of the house of Chinggis, translated from the Chinese (1829)

История первых четырех ханов из дома Чингисова

Bichurin, translator; from Yuan shi and Tongjian gangmu

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective official history (Yuan shi, 1370) in Russian translation; not eyewitness

Cautions
  • Translation from Yuan shi and Tongjian gangmu; Bichurin did not identify passages by source consistently.

Why it matters

First Russian access to the Chinese official narrative of the Mongol empire, the counterpart to Persian (Rashid al-Din) and Russian chronicle testimony.
