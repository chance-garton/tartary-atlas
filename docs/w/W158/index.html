The Jewel Translucent Sutra: the life of Altan Khan

A Mongolian court biography of Altan Khan (1508 to 1582), composed about 1607, in Elverskog's 2003 edition.

Full title The Jewel Translucent Sutra: Altan Khan and the Mongols in the Sixteenth Century (Brill 2003; Mongolian text, English translation and study)

Erdeni tunumal neretü sudur

Anonymous biographer of Altan Khan; Johan Elverskog

Open the original book

The Erdeni Tunumal Neretu Sudur is a near contemporary Mongolian biography of Altan Khan of the Tumed (1508 to 1582), composed about 1607. It follows his wars with the Ming and the Oirats and the Mongol and Tibetan alliance of 1578 that led to the title Dalai Lama. The card calls it the only substantial Mongolian narrative of the sixteenth century between the Secret History and the seventeenth century chronicles. It lists Johan Elverskog's 2003 Brill edition, with Mongolian text, English translation and study. The atlas does not hold the text of this edition: the card says the upload is of a copyrighted book, and the corpus stores no text of it, so nothing was read for this guide.

Who “Tartar” means here
Not recorded. The card names the Tumed and Ordos Mongols under Altan Khan, the Oirats, the Ming and the Tibetan Gelugpa leaders.
Why read it
It is a Mongolian account of the sixteenth century from within the court, documenting Altan Khan's wars and his alliance with Tibetan Buddhism.
Watch out
It survives in a single manuscript found in 1958, and Elverskog's edition supersedes the 1984 Beijing edition.
