The Jewel Translucent Sutra: Altan Khan and the Mongols in the Sixteenth Century (Brill 2003; Mongolian text, English translation and study)

Erdeni tunumal neretü sudur

Anonymous biographer of Altan Khan; Johan Elverskog

How we know

official or contemporary record An official document or a record made at the time.

Card wording

near-contemporary court biography (c.1607) of Altan Khan of the Tümed (1508-1582), with the Mongol-Tibetan alliance of 1578

Cautions
  • Single manuscript discovered in 1958; Elverskog's edition supersedes Jürungga 1984. Upload is of a copyrighted book.

Why it matters

Only substantial Mongolian narrative source for the 16th century between the Secret History and the 17th-century chronicles; documents Altan Khan's wars with the Ming and Oirats and the adoption of the Dalai Lama title.
