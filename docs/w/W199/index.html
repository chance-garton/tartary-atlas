The Tarikh-i Baihaki, containing the life of Mas’ud son of Sultan Mahmud of Ghazni: Persian text, Calcutta 1862

تاریخ بیهقی (تاریخ مسعودی)

Abu'l-Fazl Bayhaqi (995-1077); William Hook Morley (editor)

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand: Ghaznavid chancery secretary's memoir-history of 1030-1041, including the Seljuk Turkmen migration into Khorasan and the battle of Dandanaqan (1040)

Cautions
  • Morley's text is uncritical; superseded by Fayyaz (Mashhad 1971) and Yahaqqi and Sayyidi (Tehran 2009), Persian scans of which exist on IA (20251229_20251229_2330) but were not verified.

Why it matters

Eyewitness Persian account of the first Turkmen (Oghuz) irruption from the steppe into the Islamic lands, the event that created 'Turkestan' as a political concept in Persian writing.
