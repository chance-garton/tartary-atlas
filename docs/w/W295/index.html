Journey to the Northeast of European Russia, through the Tundras of the Samoyeds, to the Arctic Ural Mountains, undertaken in 1837 for the Imperial Botanical Garden, part 1 (Dorpat 1848)

Alexander Gustav von Schrenk

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation 1837 among the European Samoyeds (Nenets) and at the Arctic Ural, the northern boundary of Siberia

Cautions
  • Part 2 (1854) is post-cutoff publication of the same journey.
  • Low priority; included because journey date 1837 is in scope.

Why it matters

Marginal to Tartary proper but the fullest pre-1850 German ethnography of the Samoyeds on the European side of the Ural, useful for the northern limit of the peoples Strahlenberg and Georgi grouped with Siberia.
