Description of the Kirghiz-Kazakh or Kirghiz-Kaisak hordes and steppes (3 parts: geographical, historical, ethnographic; 1832)

Описание киргиз-казачьих, или киргиз-кайсацких, орд и степей

Aleksei Levshin

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

mixed: official who served at the Orenburg frontier commission 1820-1822 with informant testimony from Kazakh elders and Tatar interpreters; historical part compiled from Russian archives, Abu al-Ghazi and Chinese material via Bichurin

Cautions
  • Historical section depends on Abu al-Ghazi, Rychkov, Tevkelev and Bichurin; ethnographic section on personal enquiry.

Why it matters

The first comprehensive account of the Kazakh hordes and their history, including the Russian official account of the 1731 submission and of Kazakh-Dzungar wars; core source for a Kazakh perspective as mediated by a Russian official.
