Secret History of the Yuan Dynasty (Chinese phonetic transcription of the Secret History of the Mongols with interlinear glosses and Chinese summary)

元朝秘史

Anonymous Mongolian author(s); Ming Hanlin transcribers (c.1382-1408)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective dynastic history / epic chronicle composed within decades of the events (13th c.); survives only through the early Ming Chinese transcription

Cautions
  • The Mongolian original is lost; the transcription reflects Ming-era Chinese phonology and the Hanlin glossators' understanding. Edition of the Peking University copy is not identified in the catalogue (candidates: Ye Dehui 1908 print or an earlier manuscript); check the title page before citing. Companion: Li Wentian's 1896 commentary 元秘史注 (IA 02078742.cn ff.).
  • See separate records for de Rachewiltz 2015 (open access), Cleaves 1982 and Pelliot 1949.
  • Further copies: cdn.angkordatabase.asia/libs/docs/pelliot_hist…

Why it matters

Resolves L001 with the surviving primary form of the Secret History: the Chinese-transcribed Mongolian text is the base of every modern reconstruction (Haenisch, Pelliot, Cleaves, de Rachewiltz). It gives the Mongols' own 13th-century names for peoples, rivers and polities across the steppe.
