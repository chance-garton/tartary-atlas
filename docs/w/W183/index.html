Brief information on the Black Tatars (Heida shilüe), Russian translation, Problemy vostokovedeniia 1960 no. 5

Краткие сведения о черных татарах (Хэй-да ши-люэ)

Peng Daya and Xu Ting, Song envoys

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation by two Southern Song envoys to the Mongols 1233 and 1235-1236

Cautions
  • Text survives in later Chinese compilations; Russian translation is the only European-language full version besides Olbricht-Pinks German.

Why it matters

Chinese eyewitness ethnography of the Mongols in the 1230s using the name Black Tatars, a direct contemporary witness for the Tatar ethnonym.
