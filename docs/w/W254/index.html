History of Genghis Khan and of the whole dynasty of the Mongols his successors, conquerors of China, drawn from Chinese history

Antoine Gaubil

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of earlier sources: Gaubil, resident in Peking, worked from the Yuan shi and Chinese annals; no personal observation of Mongolia

Cautions
  • Gallica SRU returned zero hits for this title under Gaubil (catalogue spelling varies: Gentchiscan / Gentchis-Can). Complements Petis de la Croix (W077, Persian-based) and d'Ohsson.
  • Gaubil's manuscripts and letters (Correspondance de Pekin, ed. Simon 1970) are a further lead.

Why it matters

First European history of the Mongols built on Chinese rather than Persian sources; Gaubil consistently calls the steppe "la Tartarie" while distinguishing Mongous from other Tartars, so the book documents how a Sinologist in Peking mapped Chinese ethnonyms onto the European term.
