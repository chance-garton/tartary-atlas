Description of the Foreign Countries of the Western Regions (1415 embassy to Herat and Samarkand)

西域番國志 (Си юй фань го чжи)

Chen Cheng and Li Xian (Ming envoys); Russian translation by B. I. Pankratov, published 1989 and 1998

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation of the 1414-1415 Ming embassy to the Timurid court at Herat via Hami, Turfan, Samarkand

Cautions
  • Chinese text survives in Ming manuscripts (Guochao dianggu; Shuofu) and was edited by Zhou Liankuan (1991); the Xiyu xingcheng ji (itinerary) is a companion work. Chinese scan not found on IA/CADAL in this pass (see candidates).

Why it matters

Ming eyewitness of Timurid Central Asia within a decade of Clavijo (W046); gives Chinese names and descriptions of Samarkand and Herat.
