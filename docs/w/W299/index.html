J. C. Hüttner’s Report of the British Embassy’s Journey through China and a Part of Tartary (Berlin 1797)

Johann Christian Hüttner

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation: Hüttner accompanied the Macartney embassy 1792-1794 as tutor to the younger Staunton and visited Jehol (Rehe) in Inner Mongolia; published before Staunton's official account

Cautions
  • Published anonymously by Böttiger from Hüttner's letters; Hüttner later assisted Staunton. Short work.

Why it matters

An independent German eyewitness of the Macartney embassy alongside the English accounts (W057-W059, W067); the title uses Tartarei for the Jehol region beyond the Wall, an example of the late-eighteenth-century sense of Chinese Tartary.
