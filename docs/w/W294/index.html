Journey from Orenburg to Bukhara, with a vocabulary of the Afghan language, two plates and the plan of Bukhara (Berlin 1823)

Eduard Eversmann

Not read yet. This source is catalogued, but it has not been read for passages. See the sources read so far.

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

Card wording

firsthand observation: Eversmann travelled disguised as a merchant with the Negri mission caravan 1820-1821 through the Kazakh steppe to Bukhara; Klaproth contributed a linguistic note

Cautions
  • Edited by Lichtenstein from Eversmann's letters and notes; Eversmann's second attempt (1825-1826, Khiva) was never published as a book.

Why it matters

German eyewitness companion to Meyendorff (W026) on the same 1820-1821 mission to Bukhara; independent observation of the Kazakh steppe route and Bukharan society, plus the first Pashto vocabulary printed in Germany.
