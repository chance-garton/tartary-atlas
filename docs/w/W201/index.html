Card wording

mixed: the author travelled widely (Caspian shores, Iran, India) and combined observation with informant testimony and earlier books; c.943-947

Cautions
  • The Barbier de Meynard text is uncritical (Pellat's 1966-79 revision supersedes it). Volume-level IA coverage is incomplete under this search; other volumes exist under Google Books identifiers (bub_gb_dsGO2_tsVWYC etc.) not verified.

Why it matters

Tenth-century description of the Khazar-Volga-Caspian world and of the Turkic peoples east of the Caspian (Muruj chapters 17-18), including the Rus raids on the Caspian; a root text for later Islamic geography of the steppe.
