Novgorod First Chronicle, Older and Younger Redactions (1950 Academy edition)

Новгородская первая летопись старшего и младшего изводов

Anonymous Novgorod chroniclers; ed. A. N. Nasonov

How we know

official or contemporary record An official document or a record made at the time.

Card wording

contemporary annalistic entries kept in Novgorod; Kalka battle 1224, Batu invasion 1238, Tatar census 1257-1259 recorded close to events

Cautions
  • Synodal manuscript lacks the beginning (starts 1016); younger redaction draws on a lost early compilation (Shakhmatov hypothesis).

Why it matters

Only Rus chronicle with a surviving 13th-14th century manuscript; its Kalka and Batu entries are the closest Russian-language testimony to the Mongol arrival.
