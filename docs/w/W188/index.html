Cream of Histories (Zubdat al-tawarikh): Timurid history 736-830 AH (1335-1427), modern Persian edition

زبدة التواریخ

Hafiz-i Abru (d. 1430)

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

mixed: compilation of Shami, Rashid al-Din and others for the fourteenth century; firsthand court observation for Shahrukh's reign (author accompanied Timur and Shahrukh)

Cautions
  • Editor and print date inferred from the preface, not verified against a title-page image. Heavily dependent on Shami and Rashid al-Din for earlier periods.
  • Hafiz-i Abru's Geography (Jughrafiya, with its description of Turkestan and Khorasan) was not found digitised; see candidates.

Why it matters

Timurid synthesis that names the realm 'from the frontier of Khitay to the Arab lands, from farthest India to Rum'; source for the Shahrukh-era embassies and for Uzbek and Moghul affairs. Hafiz-i Abru's geography (not located online) is the other half of his work.
