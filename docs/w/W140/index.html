Imperially Commissioned Gazetteer of Shengjing (Manchuria), 130 juan, 1784 recension

欽定盛京通志

Agui and Liu Jinzhi (Qianlong commission)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

official compilation from banner records, earlier editions (1684, 1736) and surveys

Cautions
  • Late recension; earlier 1684 and 1736 editions differ in coverage. Siku quanshu (1770s-80s) manuscript copy: the Qianlong editors systematically re-transcribed Mongol, Jurchen and Khitan names following the Qinding Liao Jin Yuan san shi guoyu jie, so name forms differ from Ming and early Qing prints (seen on the Ming shi Wala chapter page: 衛拉特 for 瓦剌, 太平巴圖博囉 for 太平 and 把禿孛羅). Cite the pre-Siku editions for original orthography.

Why it matters

Qing official geography of the Manchu homeland, the region European maps called Eastern or Manchu Tartary; includes the Amur frontier with Russia and lists of tribute peoples.
