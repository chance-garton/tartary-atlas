The many Tartarys

Tartaria first appears on a map here in 1320 and last in 1836. Great Tartary reaches the maps in 1522. Chinese Tartary follows in 1652, Muscovite Tartary in 1683, Independent Tartary in 1690 and Little Tartary in 1720. Each name covered a region whose edges moved from map to map.
