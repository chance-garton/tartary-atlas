BuildingsBalkans, on the road to Constantinople1332

A queen's travelling mosque, left at the border

Ibn Battuta escorted Uzbek Khan's Byzantine wife on a visit to her father in Constantinople. Like her husband, she carried a mosque (evidently a tent) that was set up at every halt for daily prayers. At the first Byzantine fortress she left it behind, and from then on, he says with some dismay, the call to prayer stopped and wine appeared at her table.

The Queen had with her a mosque, which she set up at every stage, just as her husband used to do. In this she had daily prayers. She left it, however, at the fortress. After this the office of the Moazin ceased : wine was brought into the banquet and of this she drank.

Who Golden Horde; Byzantine princess Bayalun
