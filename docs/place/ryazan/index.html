Staraya (Old) Ryazan, the city sacked in 1237

What the sources say here: 3 passages

CitiesRyazanWinter 1237 (6745)

Riazan: through the forest in winter

The Tatars came 'through the forest' in winter, ravaged the Riazan land as far as Pronsk, burned Riazan and killed its prince. The chronicler describes what they did to captives: some were impaled or stretched, others shot with arrows, others tied with their hands behind their backs. Then the army moved on to Kolomna.

the godless Tatars came from the eastern lands onto the Riazan land, through the forest, and began to make war on the Riazan land, and captured as far as Pronsk; having captured all Riazan they burned it, and killed their prince; of those they seized, some they stretched out, others they shot through with arrows, and others they bound with their hands behind them

A sorceress envoy demands a tenth of everything

The Tatars came to Ryazan land 'without number, like locusts'. From their camp they sent envoys to the Ryazan princes, a woman sorceress and two men, demanding a tenth of everything: of people, of princes and of horses. The editor's footnote on this page adds that another copy lists the horses by colour, ten of each. The princes answered that when none of them were left, all would be theirs.

foreigners called Tatars came upon the Ryazan land, a multitude without number, like locusts … and from there they sent their envoys, a woman sorceress and two men with her, to the Ryazan princes, asking of them a tenth of everything: of people, and of princes, and of horses, a tenth of everything.

Batu's demand: a tenth of everything

Camped on the river Onuza, Batu sent envoys to the Riazan princes asking for a tenth of everything: princes, people, armour and horses. The princes answered: 'when we are gone, all will be yours.' One manuscript printed alongside says the envoys were 'a sorceress woman and two men with her', a detail that may echo Mongol shamanesses or may be later colour.

And from there they sent their envoys, a sorceress woman and two men with her, to the Riazan princes, asking of them a tenth of everything: of princes, and of people, and of armour, and of horses.
