Strange talesKakheti, Georgia1673

Amazons near the Kalmyks, and an Amazon's dress in the palace

In Georgia Chardin asked about the Amazons of classical legend. He met no one who had been to their country, but many told stories, and in the prince's palace he was shown a large woman's garment of coarse wool said to have belonged to an Amazon killed near Kakheti. The Georgian prince's son told him a numerous unknown people lived five days north, always at war with 'the Tartars, which they call Calmac'.

I confess, I never saw any Person in Georgia who had been in the Country of the Amazons; but I have heard many of 'em tell Stories of those People. And they shew'd me in the Prince's Palace, a large Female habit of a course Woollen Stuff

Who Kalmyks (as the Georgians called them) and a legendary people beyond

Heard itJean Chardin (English translation, London, 1691)

Keep in mind. Chardin reports the stories but, on the next page, rejects the classical details as Greek fables.
