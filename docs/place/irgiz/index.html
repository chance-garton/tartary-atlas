BuildingsKazakh steppe (Kowda and Irgiz rivers)as known in the 1770s

A cube-shaped tomb that nobles wanted to be buried beside

The list describes a masonry tomb of 'father' Baitak shaped like a cube, 3 to 4 fathoms across, where every Muslim prays and every noble ('white bone') wishes to be buried. It adds a stone tomb on the Irgiz said to hold Abulkhair, the Kazakh khan of the Little Horde (killed 1748), and notes the ruins of a town the Kazakhs call Dzhanket on the Syr Darya and of the 'formerly important town' Saraichik on the lower Ural.

On the Kowda (see before) is a masonry tomb of the father (ata) Baitak. It has the form of a cube 3 to 4 fathoms across. Every Muslim performs devotions here, and everyone of white bone, that is of the nobility, wants to be buried here. … On the Irgiz, at [the place of] the elder Barbi, p. 387, is a stone tomb in which Abulkhair is said to rest.

Working translation

Original wording

Am Kowda (S. vorh.) ist ein gemauert Begräbniß des Vaters (ata) Baitak. Es hat die Form eines Würfels von 3 bis 4 Faden im Durchschnitt. Jeder Muhamedaner hält hier Anbetungen, und jeder mit weißen Knochen oder von Adel, will hier begraben seyn. … Am Irgis des Acksakal Barbi S. 387. ist ein steinern Grabmahl, in welchem Abulchair ruhen soll.

Heard itJ. P. Falck, arranged and supplemented by J. G. Georgi (1785)
