Uebersichts-Karte von Ost-Hoch-Asien (Survey map of eastern High Asia), Berlin 1839

Uebersichts-Karte von Oest-Hoch-Asien ... Berlin. 1839. (Survey Map of East-High-Asia ... Berlin, 1839.)

J. L. Grimm and H. Mahlmann (Atlas von Asien to Ritter's Erdkunde), Berlin 1839

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation from published surveys and travel reports; no observation by the mapmaker.

Research notes
  • Compiled; depends on Klaproth and Jesuit material.

Why it matters

Ritter's 'Hoch-Asien' concept mapped in 1839: replaces 'Chinese Tartary' with a physical-geographic term; documents the German scholarly vocabulary Humboldt and Ritter used in the 1840s.
