Tian di tu: late-Ming woodblock atlas of the heavens, China, the provinces, Chinese Turkestan and the South Seas, 1601 (LOC Hummel copy)

[天地圖]

Zuo Junheng (fl. 17th century)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation of Ming popular geography (Guang yu tu derived) with a Western Regions sheet

Cautions
  • Date conflict: catalogue 1601 versus former call number 1639; place names labelled by hand.

Why it matters

A cheap late-Ming atlas that includes a Western Regions (Chinese Turkestan) sheet and a Jurchen north-east sheet on the eve of the Manchu rise, showing what ordinary Ming readers were told about the northern frontier.

Places named

  • China
  • Tarim Basin
  • Manchuria
  • Pacific Ocean
  • Japan

Card wording: China and 15 provinces; Chinese Turkestan; Dongbei Nüzhen (Jurchen north-east); South Seas, Japan
