Gran Tartaria divisa nelle sue parti principali ... conforme le relazioni che s’hanno dagl’itinerarij de PP. della Compagnia di Giesu, di Monsu Tavernier e dalle raccolte di Monsu Thevenot (Great Tartary divided into its principal parts, according to the Jesuit itineraries, Tavernier and Thevenot’s collections), Mercurio Geografico

Gran Tartaria divisa nelle sue parti principali : da Giacomo Cantelli, da Vignola ; conforme le relazioni, che s'hanno da gl' itinerarij de P.P. della Compa. di Giesu, di Monsu Tauernier e dalle raccolte di Monsu Theuenot. Giacomo Cantelli da Vignola; published by Giovanni Giacomo de Rossi, Rome

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation; the title itself names its sources: Jesuit itineraries (Verbiest, Martini), Tavernier, and Thevenot's collection of voyages.

Cautions
  • Compiled from Sanson and Jesuit material; the Mercurio Geografico collects Cantelli plates with variable dates (Rumsey: pagination not continuous).
  • Rumsey note: Outline hand colored Detailed map, with decorative title cartouche. Relief shown pictorially. Shows administrative divisions, cities, towns, rivers and mountains. Includes notes. Pagination is not continuous, see the index.

Why it matters

Italian state of the Sanson/Jesuit picture of Tartary with an explicit source statement in the title; useful for showing how late-17th-century compilers cited Jesuit and travellers' reports rather than any Tartar authority.
