Map room / M110
Map · Chinese

Huangchao yitong yudi quantu (Complete Map of the Unified Qing Empire), 1832 woodblock on 8 scrolls, with inset Huangchao yudi zongtu (LOC; 1842 state also held)

皇朝一統輿地全圖

Li Zhaoluo (1769-1841) and Dong Fangli

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

compilation: Li Zhaoluo's reduction of the Kangxi-Qianlong Jesuit survey atlases onto a graticule, with Daoguang-era administrative updates

Research notes
  • Based on the Qianlong Neifu yutu; LOC notes a near-identical 1842 issue.

Why it matters

The first widely printed Chinese map to place the Jesuit survey of Mongolia, Xinjiang and Tibet on a longitude-latitude grid for Chinese readers (1832), documenting Qing official geography of the northern frontier at the end of the study period.
