Siberia and Chinese Tartary (SDUK map dated 15 June 1838; 1844 volume)

Siberia and Chinese Tartary. Engraved by J. & C. Walker. Published by the Society for the Diffusion of Useful Knowledge, 59 Lincolns Inn Fields, June 15th. 1838. (London: Chapman & Hall, 1844)

Society for the Diffusion of Useful Knowledge; engraved by J. and C. Walker

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation; stated sources per Rumsey note: Piadischev (Pyadyshev) and J. Arrowsmith.

Research notes
  • Derived from Pyadyshev and Arrowsmith; not independent.
  • Rumsey note: Engraved map. Relief shown by hachures. Boundaries hand col. Sources: Piadischev and J. Arrowsmith.

Why it matters

Companion to the previous sheet: 'Chinese Tartary' is used for Qing Mongolia and Manchuria in 1838-44 British usage; together the two SDUK sheets show the tripartite Russian/Independent/Chinese Tartary scheme still in print in the 1840s.
