Map room / M115
Map · Chinese

Manuscript silk scroll of four Qing frontier maps: Aihun (Amur), Luosha (Russians), Taiwan and Inner Mongolia, c.1697 (LOC)

[璦琿 羅刹 臺灣 內蒙古圖]

anonymous Qing court painter

How we know

official or contemporary record An official document or a record made at the time.

official record: Kangxi-era military-administrative pictorial maps of the Amur frontier with the Russians (Luosha) after the Nerchinsk settlement, of Taiwan and of Inner Mongolia

Research notes
  • Undated; LOC assigns c.1697 from content. Pictorial bird's-eye style, no scale.

Why it matters

A Qing internal view of the Amur frontier and the Russians (named Luosha) in the 1690s, the Chinese counterpart to Remezov's Amur leaf and to Witsen; directly relevant to how the Qing mapped the northern neighbours.
