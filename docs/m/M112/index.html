Map · Chinese · Islamic, Russian, East Asian and Inner Asian cartography

Cheonhado (Korean wheel-map of the world) and Ch’onha chido atlas, manuscript copies c.1800 (two LOC items)

天下圖 / 天下地圖

anonymous Korean copyists

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

retrospective compilation: Choson-era popular world map combining Chinese classical geography (Shanhaijing lands) with a China-centred inner continent; peoples of the north appear as classical names

Cautions
  • Undated copies; LOC dates c.1800 by style. The wheel map tradition has no single author.

Why it matters

Shows the Korean vernacular picture of Inner Asia around 1800: a China-centred continent where the Mongol and Jurchen north is framed in classical terms, with no Tartary concept; a control case for the East Asian side.
