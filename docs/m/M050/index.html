Tartaria sive Magni Chami Imperium (Tartary, or the Empire of the Great Khan), Atlas Maior 1665

Tartaria sive Magni Chami Imperivm

Joan Blaeu (plate first issued by Willem and Joan Blaeu c.1640)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation: Blaeu's plate follows the Hondius model with a new decorative cartouche (camel, Tartar figures); no observation.

Research notes
  • Not independent: copied from Hondius 1606; the Jansson Tartaria of 1636-1658 is a parallel copy. Same plate reissued in Latin, French, Dutch, German and Spanish Atlas Maior editions 1662-1672.
  • Rumsey note: 120 Milliaria Germanica communia = 6.8 cm

Why it matters

The most widely circulated mid-17th-century image of 'Tartaria': it shows the size and vagueness of the term (from the Volga to the Pacific, north of China and the Mughal empire) just before Witsen's 1687 map; the Rumsey copy is the 1665 Atlas Maior state. This is the plate the IA item 'dr_tartaria-sive-magni-chami-imperium...' is often confused with; that IA item is actually the Allard/Witsen plate (see separate record).
