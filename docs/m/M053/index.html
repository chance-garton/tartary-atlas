Carte Nouvelle de la Grande Tartarie (New map of Great Tartary), Atlas Nouveau, 1708 issue

Carte Nouvelle de la Grande Tartarie.

Alexis-Hubert Jaillot, after Nicolas Sanson

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation: enlarged re-engraving of Sanson's 1652 Grande Tartarie with updates; no observation.

Cautions
  • Derivative of Sanson 1652; the Mortier (Amsterdam) 1708 issue is a pirated/licensed re-engraving of Jaillot's Paris plates.

Why it matters

Shows the French Sanson-Jaillot state of Tartary at the moment Delisle (M007, 1706) replaced it; the two side by side document the shift from compiled tradition to Russian-informed mapping.
