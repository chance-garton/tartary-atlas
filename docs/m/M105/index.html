Yu ji tu (Map of the Tracks of Yu), grid map on the 1136 Xi’an stele: rubbings at LOC (Hummel) and via WDL (National Library of China)

禹跡圖

anonymous (Song), stele carved 1136

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

compilation of Song administrative geography on a 100-li grid (5,110 squares)

Research notes
  • Two rubbings (LOC and NLC) of the same stele; LOC rubbing date given as 1933?

Why it matters

Reference object for Chinese grid mapping in 1136; establishes the domestic frame against which the Hua yi tu treats the north as foreign; limited direct bearing on Tartary.
