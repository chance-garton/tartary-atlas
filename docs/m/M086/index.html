Tabula Tartariae et majoris partis Regni Chinae (Map of Tartary and the greater part of the Kingdom of China), De Wit atlas c.1682

Tabula Tartariae et majoris partis Regni Chinae.

Frederick de Wit, Amsterdam

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation; no observation by the mapmaker.

Cautions
  • Derived from Martini/Blaeu China and the Blaeu Tartaria; parallel Uppsala copy on IA (arkivkopia.se-ublu-22274, dated 1662). Not independent.
  • Rumsey note: Date estimated.

Why it matters

Post-Manchu-conquest Dutch map pairing Tartary with China (cf. W094, W099) before Witsen: shows the Dutch trade's 'Tartaria' as the Manchu-Mongol north of the Wall in the 1680s.
