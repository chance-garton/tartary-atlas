Ost Siberien, Mongolei, Mandschurei (East Siberia, Mongolia, Manchuria), sheet of the Atlas von Asien in zwanzig Blaettern, c.1833 (1840 set)

Ost Siberien. Mongolei. Mandschire. (East Siberia. Mongolia. Manchuria).

J. L. Grimm and H. Mahlmann (Atlas von Asien to Carl Ritter's Erdkunde), Berlin

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation from published surveys and travel reports; no observation by the mapmaker.

Cautions
  • Rumsey note: duplicate of previous map in the set; date estimated. Grimm's sheets rest on Klaproth's Asia polyglotta and Russian mapping.
  • Rumsey note: Date estimated based on other dated maps in set. Duplicate of previous map.

Why it matters

The scholarly (Ritter school) map of the region c.1833-40: no Tartary, but Mongolei and Mandschurei as physical-ethnographic units, drawn from Klaproth, Jesuit surveys and Russian sources; the academic counterpart to the SDUK's 'Chinese Tartary'.
