Map of the Far East and adjacent Pacific (LOC devised title), pen and ink, ‘Rossi’ Marco Polo map

Attributed in the sheet to Marco Polo (Rossi collection); authenticity undetermined

How we know

claimed or disputed Authorship, date or authenticity is claimed or contested.

Card wording

Disputed/claimed: sheet claims Marco Polo authorship; LOC catalogue states 'Undetermined authenticity' and 'Provenance unverified'; no accepted observation date.

Cautions
  • LOC notes: 'Undetermined authenticity'; 'Provenance unverified'; further description in Leo Bagrow, 'The maps from the home archives of the descendants of a friend of Marco Polo', Imago Mundi 5 (1948). The Rossi collection sheets are examined in Benjamin B. Olshin, The Mysteries of the Marco Polo Maps (University of Chicago Press, 2014), which sets out arguments for and against their authenticity and reaches no firm conclusion; this inventory records the catalogue status only.
  • Lead L005 closed as a disputed object with the catalogue caveats copied verbatim.

This record carries a published correction. See Corrections.

Why it matters

Record only as a disputed object: it uses the phrase 'Cattigara of Tartary' in a key purportedly by Polo, but the LOC itself flags undetermined authenticity and unverified provenance. Not to be cited as medieval evidence without the Bagrow 1948 and later analyses.
