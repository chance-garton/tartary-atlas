Carta marina (Marine chart of the Portuguese navigations and of the whole known world), 12 woodcut sheets, 1516

Carta marina navigatoria Portugallen navigationes atque tocius cogniti orbis terre marisque formam naturam situs et terminos nostris temporibus recognitos et ab antiquorum traditione differentes ... hec generaliter indicat

Martin Waldseemueller (with Johann Schoener's annotations in this copy)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation: Portuguese marine charts for coasts; for inner Asia a fusion of Ptolemy, Marco Polo and medieval legend (Terra Mongal / vera Tartaria with the enthroned Khan); no observation.

Cautions
  • Sole surviving copy, annotated by Johann Schoener; LOC also holds a manuscript copy of sheet 6 (item 2016586443). Inner-Asian content derives from Polo and Ptolemy rather than any 16th-century report; later Fries 1525 reduced copies exist.
  • Found via LOC JSON search 'Carta marina Waldseemuller 1516' (2 hits).

Why it matters

Earliest printed map in the inventory to name Tartaria in the modern sense: sheet 3 carries TARTARIA across the steppe and the heading TERRA MONGAL ET QUE VERA TARTARIA DICITUR (Land of the Mongols, which is called true Tartary) with the enthroned Khan, alongside Cumania and Great Bulgaria, showing the 1516 equation Mongol land = Tartary and its placement north of Cathay/Sina.
