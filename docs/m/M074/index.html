Asien, 1849 (Meyer’s Zeitung Atlas no. 58; Neuester Zeitungs-Atlas 1852)

Asien, gez. v. Hauptm. Radefeld. 1849. Stich, Druck und Verlag der geographischen Anstalt des Bibliographischen Instituts zu Hildburghausen. In stahl gest. v. C. Metzeroth. (In upper margin) Meyer's Zeitung Atlas. No. 58. (to accompany) Neuester Zeitungs-Atlas fur Alte und Neue Erdkunde. Herausgegeb

Joseph Meyer (Bibliographisches Institut, Hildburghausen); drawn by Capt. Radefeld; engraved by C. Metzeroth

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation from published surveys and travel reports; no observation by the mapmaker.

Research notes
  • Compiled; derivative of Stieler.
  • Rumsey note: Engraved outline hand color map, color coded by regions and European possession. Shows political boundaries, cities and towns. Relief shown by hachures.

Why it matters

German popular atlas sheet of 1849 to close the gap: shows Meyer's mass-market usage of regional names at the end of the 1840s.
