Qing neifu yuditu suo moben: reduced manuscript copy of the Qianlong Imperial Household atlas (Neifu yutu), 1821, National Central Library Taipei via WDL

清內府輿地圖縮摹本

Liu Yan; Weng Tonghe (contributor)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

copy: reduction of the 1760s thirteen-row copperplate Qianlong atlas (Neifu yutu) by a Qing geographer

Research notes
  • A reduced copy, so scale and some names are simplified; provenance via Weng Tonghe. Not the copperplate itself.

Why it matters

An accessible witness to the Qianlong Neifu yutu, the Qing survey atlas that first mapped Xinjiang and the Kazakh borderlands after 1759; the original copperplate atlas is not open online.
