New Map of Lake Baikal, surveyed during its circumnavigation in 1772 and 1773 (from Bemerkungen einer Reise, vol. 1, 1775)

Johann Gottlieb Georgi; engraver A. Rykov

How we know

firsthand The author saw the places or people described.

firsthand survey by Georgi 1772-1773

Why it matters

First survey-based map of Lake Baikal, replacing the schematic Baikal of earlier Tartary maps.
