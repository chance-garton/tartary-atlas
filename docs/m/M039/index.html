Catalan Estense world map (Catalan planisphere), Modena, Biblioteca Estense C.G.A.1

Mappamondo catalano estense

Anonymous Catalan (Majorcan) cartographer

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation: Catalan portolan tradition with Asian interior from Polo-era and 15th-century Latin sources; no observation.

Research notes
  • Anonymous; content descends from the Catalan Atlas tradition and Catalan portolans. Estense Digital Library record could not be opened from this environment.
  • Sampled through the Museo Galileo 'Il mappamondo di Fra Mauro' online exhibition (2021-2025), which serves zoomify tile pyramids of comparison maps by concession of the holding libraries (site states further reproduction forbidden). Tile base: mostre.museogalileo.it/images/framauro/05_mapp…; tiles at TileGroupN/level-col-row.jpg.

Why it matters

Mid-15th-century Catalan successor of the Catalan Atlas showing how the Asian interior (Caspian, steppe cities, seated Tartar/Mongol rulers) was redrawn after the Timurid period; a bridge between the 1375 atlas and the printed Ptolemies.
