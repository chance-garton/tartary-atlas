Why it matters

Mid-15th-century Catalan successor of the Catalan Atlas showing how the Asian interior (Caspian, steppe cities, seated Tartar/Mongol rulers) was redrawn after the Timurid period; a bridge between the 1375 atlas and the printed Ptolemies.
