Catalogue / M083
Map · Dutch · European cartography

New land map of the northern and eastern part of Asia and Europe, extending from Nova Zembla to China (Witsen’s map, dated 1687)

Nieuwe Lantkaarte van het Noorder en Ooster deel van Asia en Europa strekkende van Nova Zemla tot China

Nicolaes Witsen, Amsterdam

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation from Russian informants, Siberian route descriptions, Chinese-Jesuit and embassy sources gathered by Witsen; no observation by Witsen beyond his 1664-65 Moscow journey (see W079, L009).

Cautions
  • Several states (1687 date on the plate; issues c.1690 and 1705 with corrections); the Fotothek object is catalogued only as 'Karte von Asien und Europa, Kupferstich, 1687' and is one sheet. Gallica gives 1687 for all four arks. The Allard c.1697 plate (Rumsey 11647126) is a reduced derivative.
  • Europeana 'Witsen Tartarye kaart' gave 0 hits; the title search 'Nieuwe Lantkaarte' gave 7. Gallica SRU dc.creator Witsen gave 27 hits including a 1687 manuscript copy of the Yaik course 'copiee de celle de M. Vitzen' (btv1b55003029k).

Why it matters

The map announced in W079 (Philosophical Transactions 1686) and behind L009: the first map of Siberia built on Russian material, the source of the Allard/Van der Aa reduction recorded earlier, and the reason 'Tartary' begins to fragment into named peoples on European maps after 1690.
