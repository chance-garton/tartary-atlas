Die Voelker Asiens und Europas (The peoples of Asia and Europe), Physikalischer Atlas, 8th section, Ethnographie no. 1, 1847

Die Volker Asien's Und Europa's. 8te Abtheilung: Ethnographie No 1. Gotha, bei Justus Perthes. 1847.

Heinrich Berghaus, Gotha (Justus Perthes)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation: ethnographic-linguistic synthesis (Klaproth, Ritter, Humboldt) mapped by language families; no observation.

Cautions
  • Compiled from Klaproth's Asia polyglotta and Ritter; a synthesis, not a survey. Rumsey copy is the 1849 issue of the atlas.
  • Rumsey note: A large full color map of Europe and Asia showing the ethnographic distribution of people with an accompanying chart.

Why it matters

Fills the 1845-48 gap with the map that replaced 'Tartary' by ethnolinguistic classification: Berghaus colours peoples rather than a Tartar region, which is the scientific vocabulary the presentation can contrast with the popular atlases of the same years.
