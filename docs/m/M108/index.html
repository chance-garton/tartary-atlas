Der Jesuiten-Atlas der Kanghsi-Zeit: China und die Aussenlaender (Peking 1941-43): facsimile of the 35 sheets of the Kangxi atlas incl. Mukden, Mongolia, Hami and Tibet

皇輿全覽圖

Walter Fuchs, editor; J.-B. Régis, P. Jartoux and the Jesuit surveyors; Kangxi emperor

How we know

mixed Part firsthand observation, part drawn from other sources.

Card wording

official survey 1708-1718; facsimile edition of the 1721 copperplate state with Fuchs's commentary

Cautions
  • Facsimile of the copperplate state; sheet titles per Fuchs; the atlas itself is in Beijing, London (BL) and Kyoto. Post-1850 publication but reproduces 1721 material (eligible under the brief).

Why it matters

The most accessible complete reproduction of the Kangxi atlas, the first instrument-surveyed map of Mongolia and Manchuria (1717-21), i.e. the Qing official picture of the lands that European maps labelled Chinese Tartary; the 1721 state is what d'Anville redrew.
