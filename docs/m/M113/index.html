Chikyu zenzu (Complete Map of the Earth), two hemispheres, 1792: hand-drawn copy of the first Japanese copperplate world map (LOC)

地球全圖

Shiba Kokan (1747-1818)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation: Japanese redaction of Dutch (Valck/Covens and Mortier) hemispheres

Cautions
  • LOC copy is a manuscript replica, not an impression; authenticity of the paper noted by LOC.

Why it matters

First Japanese copperplate world map (1792), marking the shift from the Ricci frame (Nagakubo) to Dutch outlines of northern Asia; useful to compare how the Tartary/Siberia labels changed in Japan between 1785 and 1816.

Card wording: World; Asia with Tartary and Siberia as in Dutch sources
