Old maps / M033
Map · Latin · French and Dutch

New map of the Russian Empire drawn from the most accurate maps hitherto, especially from the map of Nicolaes Witsen (route map of the Ides embassy to China, 1692-1695)

Evert Ysbrants Ides; Nicolaes Witsen; Gerrit Drogenham

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation: reduction of Witsen 1687 map with the Ides embassy route added; issued with the Dutch Ides 1704 and later

Cautions
  • Gallica attributes different states to Ides and to Witsen; e-rara catalogue dates 1673 and 1720 bracket rather than date the sheet. Record both attributions.
  • Related to W006 (Ides English 1706). The Dutch Ides text (Driejaarige reize, 1704) not located in this pass: see candidates.

Why it matters

Links the English pass W006 (Ides travels) to Witsen's cartography: the map that carried Witsen's Siberia and the Ides route into eighteenth-century atlases. Useful for tracing how Witsen's toponymy for Tartary spread.
