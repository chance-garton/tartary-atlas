Map room / M090
Map · German

Terrestrial globe, the so-called Behaim Globe (Erdapfel), Nuremberg 1492

Erdglobus, sogenannter Behaim-Globus (Erdapfel)

Martin Behaim (concept), Georg Glockendon (painter), Nuremberg 1492-1494

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation: Ptolemy, Marco Polo, Mandeville and Portuguese reports assembled in Nuremberg; Asia (Cathay, Tartar realms, Great Khan) drawn from Polo; no observation.

Research notes
  • Content derives from Polo, Mandeville and Ptolemy (Nuremberg humanist circle); the GNM's 2023 digitisation (Behaim digital) offers unrolled high-resolution views that were not reached from this environment. Not an independent witness.
  • GNM page reachable (HTTP 200); the 'Behaim digital' 3D viewer was not located by URL.

Why it matters

The last pre-Columbian picture of Asia in Europe: Behaim's globe keeps the Polo-derived Great Khan and Tartar Asia at the moment printed Ptolemies were reviving Scythia; useful as the 1492 control between Walsperger/Fra Mauro and the Waldseemueller maps.
