Carte Physique et Politique de l'Asie.

Adrien-Hubert Brue (original); E. Andriveau-Goujon, Paris

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation from published surveys and travel reports; no observation by the mapmaker.

Research notes
  • Brue plate updated by Andriveau-Goujon; compiled.

Why it matters

French 1850 continental sheet for the end of the period; Brue/Andriveau-Goujon nomenclature to compare with British 'Tartary' titles of the same year.
