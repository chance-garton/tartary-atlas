L’Asie depuis la mort de Tchinghiz-Khan (Gengis Khan) jusqu’a l’epoque de la preponderance des Anglais, 1227 a 1840 (Asia from the death of Genghis Khan to the era of British preponderance), historical atlas map III, 1844

L'Asie depuis la mort de Tchinghiz-Khan (Gengis Khan) jusqu'a l'epoque de la preponderance des Anglais 1227 a 1840. Dressee sous la direction de A. Houze. (In upper margin:) Atlas historique et geographique de l'Asie. Carte III. (Paris, Chez P. Dumenil, editeur, rue des Beaux-Arts, 10. 1844)

Antoine Philippe Houze, Paris

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Retrospective history: an 1844 historical map reconstructing the Mongol empire and successor states; no contemporary observation.

Research notes
  • Retrospective; based on d'Ohsson and Pétis de la Croix-type histories (W077).
  • Rumsey note: Hand colored map. Explanation in lower margin. Prime meridian: Paris.

Why it matters

Labelled supplement: shows how an 1844 French historical atlas periodised 'Tartar' Asia from Genghis Khan onward, useful for distinguishing 19th-century retrospective reconstruction from contemporary mapping in the presentation.
