Old maps / M079
Map · Latin · European cartography

New and enlarged description of the world adapted for the use of navigators (Mercator’s 1569 world map on 18 sheets)

Nova et aucta orbis terrae descriptio ad usum navigantium emendate accomodata

Gerard Mercator, Duisburg

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation: Mercator's own synthesis of Ptolemy, Polo, Jenkinson (1562, for Russia and the Caspian steppe) and Portuguese charts, with long explanatory legends; no observation.

Cautions
  • Only three complete copies survive (Basel, Paris, Rotterdam); the Basel copy is the one sampled. Northern Asia depends on Jenkinson 1562 and Polo; legends are Mercator's own commentary.
  • Found via Gallica SRU (2 hits) which also pointed to e-rara.

Why it matters

The 1569 world map is the hinge between the Ptolemaic Scythia and the atlas Tartaria: Mercator's northern Asia carries the Great Khan, Tartar tribes and Jenkinson's steppe names with legends explaining his sources, and it is the direct ancestor of the 1595 Atlas Asia and of the Hondius Tartaria (see Rumsey 10534343 record).
