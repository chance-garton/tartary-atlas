New description of Great Tartary, the Empire of the Great Mogul, Japan and China

Magnae Tartariae, Magni Mogolis Imperii, Iaponiae et Chinae nova descriptio

Frederick de Wit, Amsterdam

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation: De Wit's plate combines the Blaeu/Jansson Tartaria model with Martini's 1655 China and Jesuit Japan; no observation.

Cautions
  • Derived from Blaeu 'Tartaria sive Magni Chami Imperium' and Martini; similar plates by Visscher, Danckerts and Schenk are not independent witnesses.
  • Found via LOC search 'Tartariae' (3 hits).

Why it matters

Representative of the late-17th-century Dutch 'Magna Tartaria' plates that repeated the Blaeu/Jansson model until Witsen's 1687 map replaced it; useful as the pre-Witsen state of knowledge.
