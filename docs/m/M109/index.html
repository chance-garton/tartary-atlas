Da Qing wannian yitong tianxia quantu (Complete Map of the Everlasting Unified Great Qing Empire), 1811 woodblock on 8 scrolls (LOC Hummel copy); with the 1796-1820 state via WDL

大清萬年一統天下全圖

Huang Qianren (1694-1771), original 1767; 1811 woodblock re-issue

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

compilation: Huang Qianren's 1767 map after Huang Zongxi, updated with Qianlong-era conquests (Xinjiang) and surrounding states from tributary knowledge

Cautions
  • Many states 1767-1860; LOC holds 1811 and 1814, WDL/NLC the 1796-1820 revision with additions in blue. Huang Zongxi's 1673 map is the ultimate source.
  • Retry the IIIF region request; LOC tile server returned 503 on two large-region requests during this session.

Why it matters

The best-known late-Qing popular map of the empire: it fixes the Qing view of Mongolia, Xinjiang and the Kazakh and Russian neighbours around 1800 in Chinese terms, the domestic counterpart to Western Chinese Tartary maps of the same decades.
