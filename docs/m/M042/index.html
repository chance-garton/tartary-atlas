Andrea Bianco’s circular world map, 1436, in his portolan atlas, Marciana Ms. It. Z. 76 (=4783), ff. 10v-11r

Mappamondo (in the atlas of Andrea Bianco, 1436)

Andrea Bianco (Venetian galley officer and chartmaker)

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation: portolan charts (firsthand Mediterranean navigation) plus a schematic mappamundi from medieval sources; Asia not observed.

Cautions
  • Signed and dated; the mappamundi is schematic and depends on medieval sources, while the same atlas contains a separate Ptolemaic world map (fol. 9v-10r per literature, not sampled).
  • Sampled through the Museo Galileo 'Il mappamondo di Fra Mauro' online exhibition (2021-2025), which serves zoomify tile pyramids of comparison maps by concession of the holding libraries (site states further reproduction forbidden). Tile base: mostre.museogalileo.it/images/framauro/05_mapp…; tiles at TileGroupN/level-col-row.jpg.

Why it matters

Bianco is named by the Museo Galileo exhibition as a cartographic source of Fra Mauro (M027); his 1436 mappamundi represents the Venetian image of Tartar Asia a generation before Fra Mauro and in the same city.
