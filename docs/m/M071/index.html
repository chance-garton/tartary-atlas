Carte de la Siberie ou Russie d’Asie (Map of Siberia or Asiatic Russia), 1832, Atlas universel 1833

Carte de la Siberie ou Russie d'Asie. Dressee par Mr. Lapie, Lt. Colonel et Mr. Lapie fils, Capitaine d'Etat Major. Paris, 1832. Chez Eymery Fruger et Cie., Rue Mazarine No. 30. Gravee par Lallemand.

Pierre Lapie and Alexandre Emile Lapie; engraved by Lallemand, Paris

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Compilation from published surveys and travel reports; no observation by the mapmaker.

Research notes
  • Compiled from Russian Academy and Pyadyshev maps.
  • Rumsey note: Engraved map. Outline hand color. Relief shown by hachures. Prime meridian: Paris.

Why it matters

French 1832 state that titles the region 'Siberie ou Russie d'Asie' rather than Tartarie, forty years after Bonne (M015) still used Tartarie; a French data point for the terminological shift before the 1840s.
