Map of the Russian Empire and of all Tartary, both the Greater Asiatic and the Lesser European, compiled from the most recent and approved records and reports

Cautions
  • Derived from Strahlenberg 1730 and Kirilov 1734 (the title says 'from the most recent records'); issue dates vary by copy (1739 vs 1759). Not independent. IA catalogue title contains OCR errors ('Unibversae', 'probantissimis').
  • Found via IA search creator:Homann title:Russi* (10 hits) and Europeana 'Imperii Russici et Tartariae universae' (6 hits).

Why it matters

The standard German-atlas map (1739-1760s) that packages Strahlenberg's Siberia and Kirilov's Russia under the double heading 'Russian Empire and all Tartary, greater Asiatic and lesser European', the clearest printed statement of the 18th-century distinction between Great Tartary (Asia) and Little Tartary (Crimea).
