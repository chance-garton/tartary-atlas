Asia, newly engraved 1844 (Stieler’s Hand-Atlas, 1845 issue)

Asia : entw. gez. von F. v. Stülpnagel. Neu gestochen 1844.

Friedrich von Stuelpnagel (drawn); Adolf Stieler's Hand-Atlas, Justus Perthes, Gotha

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

Card wording

Compilation from published surveys and travel reports; no observation by the mapmaker.

Cautions
  • Compiled at Perthes from Russian, British and Humboldt/Ritter material.
  • Rumsey note: Map of Asia.

Why it matters

German 1844 continental sheet: uses Sibirien, Turan, Iran and Mongolei with no 'Tartarei' at all, showing that the Perthes school had abandoned the term a decade before the British popular atlases.
