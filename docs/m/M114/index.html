Map room / M114
Map · Japanese

Shintei bankoku zenzu (Newly Revised Map of All Countries), 1816, two sheets: National Diet Library copy via WDL

新訂萬國全圖

Takahashi Kageyasu (1785-1829), with Hazama Shigetomi and Baba Sajuro; etched by Aodo Denzen

How we know

compilation Assembled from earlier sources. The author did not observe it directly.

official compilation by the shogunal astronomy office from Arrowsmith and other European maps plus Japanese surveys

Research notes
  • WDL description via NDL; multiple states exist (1810 manuscript, 1816 print).

Why it matters

The most accurate Japanese world map before Perry; its northern Asia follows Arrowsmith and Krusenstern, so it records how Japanese officialdom in 1816 named the regions Europeans called Tartary.

Places named

  • Whole world
  • North Asia
  • Siberia
  • Tartary
  • Manchuria
  • Sakhalin
  • Ezo (Hokkaido)

World; northern Asia with Siberia, Tartary, Manchuria, Sakhalin, Ezo
