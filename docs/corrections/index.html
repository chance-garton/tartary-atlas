Corrections

Corrections and new sources

The atlas corrects itself in public. If a date, place, reading or link is wrong, or a source is missing, send it in and the fix will be logged below with its evidence.

How to send a correction

  • Open an issue on the atlas repository.
  • Give the record ID (for example W122 or M081), what is wrong, and a link to a source that shows it.
  • To suggest a new source, give its title, date, and a link to a scan or catalogue entry.

Corrections log

  1. 2026-09-29 · C001

    M029: The Olshin book on the Rossi "Marco Polo" maps was published in 2014, not 2019, and it leaves the question of authenticity open rather than calling the sheets copies or fabrications.

    Was: “The Rossi collection sheets have been examined (2019 monograph by Benjamin B. Olshin) with the conclusion that they are post-medieval copies or fabrications; this inventory records the catalogue status only.”

    Now: “The Rossi collection sheets are examined in Benjamin B. Olshin, The Mysteries of the Marco Polo Maps (University of Chicago Press, 2014), which sets out arguments for and against their authenticity and reaches no firm conclusion; this inventory records the catalogue status only.”

    • University of Chicago Press: The Mysteries of the Marco Polo Maps (2014)
    • Western Association of Map Libraries review, Information Bulletin 46(3)

    M029: The dating note said scholarship treats the Rossi sheets as much later productions. Scholarship is divided (see C001), so the note now rests on the Library of Congress catalogue status alone.

    Was: “scholarship treats them as much later productions, so no date is plotted”
