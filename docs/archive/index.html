The archive

The catalogue and the working tools behind the passages. Come here when you want to check a date, trace a map, or see everything at once.

  • Old mapsPictures of 89 of the 119 maps, in date order.
  • The full catalogueAll 303 written sources and 119 maps in date order, with evidence class, cautions and access links. Searchable by name, place and ID.
  • PlacesA gazetteer of 356 places the records name, with how each map point was chosen.
  • Peoples and polities138 peoples and polities named in the records, grouped into families.
  • Tartary labelsA timeline of when Tartary, Great, Little, Chinese, Independent and Muscovite Tartary appear in the records.
  • What “Tartar” meantWhich peoples the word pointed to, source by source and century by century.
  • Map lineageWhich maps copied, reissued or corrected which.
  • MethodHow sources were dated, places plotted, passages chosen and quotes checked, and what the atlas does not claim.
  • CorrectionsHow to send a correction, and the public log of corrections made.
